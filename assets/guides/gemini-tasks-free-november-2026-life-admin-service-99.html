Gemini Tasks Is Free Now — Sell It as a $99/Month Life-Admin Service

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 26, 2026 · 6 min read · Updated on November 26, 2026

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: Google made Gemini Tasks free on November 17, 2026, for users in the U.S., Canada, Australia, Japan, Korea, and India. Tasks is a mode inside the Gemini app that calls businesses, books appointments, audits bills, and pays for things through Google Pay — you hand it an outcome and it does the work. The money angle is selling the setup as a life-admin service to busy people, around $99 a month. The honest catch: the tool is free, so you are selling your time and follow-through, not the tool — and you still have to find the clients.

What Gemini Tasks Actually Does

Tasks is a toggle inside the Gemini app: switch from Chat to Task, and Gemini stops just answering questions and starts finishing jobs. It runs on Gemini 4 Hydrogen and used to be paid-only for Google AI Ultra subscribers. Google's own launch examples cover four kinds of work:

  • Phone calls — it calls businesses, checking hours, asking about availability, or booking an appointment, and identifies itself as AI right away. In Google's demo it called two Honda dealerships at once, sat through the hold music, and booked a Friday morning service slot in under five minutes.
  • Bookings — it finds a free time in your Google Calendar, books the appointment, and adds it to the calendar.
  • Bill checks — given access through Google Password Manager, it can compare utility bills and spot patterns; in one example it found a heat pump running during peak pricing.
  • Payments — through Google's Agent Payments Protocol (AP2), it can compare products, apply a discount code, and check out with Google Pay inside a spending cap you set. You approve every purchase before it goes through.

How the $99/Month Service Works

  1. Learn it in an afternoon. Run the jobs on your own life first — book your own haircut, audit your own electric bill. You need to know where it stalls before you sell it to anyone.
  2. Pick one client type. Busy parents, small business owners, and real-estate agents all share one problem: a to-do list full of phone calls they never make. Pick one group and speak to their exact chores, not "AI tasks" in general.
  3. Sell the setup, not the button. Charge for connecting their Gmail, Calendar, and Google Pay, setting spending caps, and building their recurring routines — plus a monthly retainer for the tasks that come in. The $99/month figure floating around this week is a reasonable starting price: a few scheduled tasks a week plus one bill audit a month.
  4. Do the follow-through yourself. Gemini books the appointment, but you confirm it, handle the exception when a business says no, and report back. That follow-through is the service. The AI does the calling; you do the owning.

The Honest Timeline

  • Week 1: Learn Tasks on your own errands. Cost: $0.
  • Weeks 2–3: Offer a free setup to one or two people you know, in exchange for a testimonial and a referral. This is how you learn what breaks.
  • Month 2: Two or three paying clients at $99 a month is $200–$300 a month — real money, not a full income.
  • Months 3–6: Ten clients at $99 a month is about $1,000 a month. Reaching that means finding 10 busy people who trust you with their calendar and their card — the trust is the hard part, not the tool.

The Catches Nobody Mentions

  • Free to use does not mean free to sell. Your client can open the same free app. They pay you because they will not set it up themselves and they want someone accountable when it goes wrong.
  • Calling has limits. The phone features need a U.S. number and exclude Indiana, Louisiana, Minnesota, Montana, and Nebraska. Businesses can opt out of AI calls whenever they want.
  • Payments are U.S.-first. The AP2 checkout and deeper Google Pay integration are rolling out in the U.S. first. Elsewhere, the shopping features may not be available yet.
  • This is a service, not passive income. Ten clients at $99 a month is real work — it just pays better per hour than most gig apps.

FAQ

Q: Is Gemini Tasks really free?
A: Yes — since November 17, 2026, Tasks is free in the U.S., Japan, Korea, Canada, Australia, and India. It was previously limited to paid Google AI Ultra subscribers. Some of the newest capabilities have separate availability restrictions, and the advanced payment features are U.S.-first.

Q: Do I need coding skills to sell this service?
A: No. Tasks is a toggle inside the Gemini app — you type the outcome in plain language. The skills that matter are organization, communication, and reliability: knowing what to ask for, checking the result, and telling the client what happened.

Q: Can Gemini Tasks really make phone calls for me?
A: Yes. It can call businesses to check hours, ask about availability, or book appointments, and it tells the business it is an AI assistant. It will not share your payment or private account information unless you say so, and you approve purchases before they happen.
