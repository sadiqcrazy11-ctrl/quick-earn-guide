What to Do Before the Wave Ends

  1. Buy the confirmed lows today, not tomorrow night. Stock on the best prices goes first — the S25 Plus at $680 is already flagged as unlikely to last. Prices end November 15 (some listings expire in the early hours of November 16).
  2. Check the price on the buy button before you order. Deal prices change by the hour, and a listing can sell out and come back at a higher price.
  3. Don't buy a "maybe" deal. If the price is not clearly a low — or the item is not something you already wanted — wait. Wave two (November 23–29) and Cyber Monday (November 30–December 2) bring the deepest cuts of the season.
  4. Remember the no-adjustment rule. Walmart.com offers no price adjustments. If you buy now and the price drops in wave two, you do not get the difference back. That is the whole reason to buy only the confirmed lows now and wait on everything else.

Honest Timeline: What Happens Next

  • November 15: Wave 1 ends. The prices above go away.
  • November 23–29: Wave 2 (Black Friday Deals) — the deepest cuts of the season land here.
  • November 30–December 2: Cyber Monday, online only.
  • November 19–30: Amazon's Black Friday Week runs in parallel, so the same products may be cheaper there by next week.

If you miss the S25 Plus at $680, do not panic-buy it at a higher price on Monday. Wave two is eight days away, and Amazon's sale starts November 19.
