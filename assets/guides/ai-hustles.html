AI Hustles & ChatGPT Prompts for Cash

By Sadiq Khan · Published October 2026 · 3 min read

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: Pick ONE small digital product, build it with ChatGPT and Canva in a weekend, list it on Etsy or Gumroad, and spend the rest of the week promoting it and pitching AI services on Fiverr/Upwork. Honest timeline: a first sale typically takes 1–4 weeks, and marketplaces keep roughly 20% in fees.

The 7-day plan

  1. Day 1 — Pick one product. An e-book, social-media template pack, or Canva design set for ONE niche you understand (students, realtors, small cafés).
  2. Day 2 — Draft with ChatGPT. Outline first, then draft section by section. Rewrite in your own voice — raw AI text does not sell twice.
  3. Day 3 — Design it in Canva. Clean cover, readable pages, one consistent style. Ugly products get refunded; tidy ones get shared.
  4. Day 4 — List it for sale. Etsy or Gumroad. Title it with the words buyers search, price it $9–$29, and remember the ~20% fee when you price.
  5. Day 5 — Post 3 social posts. Show the problem, the product, and one page inside it. No posting, no sales — the marketplace alone won't find you.
  6. Day 6 — Pitch AI services. List one service on Fiverr/Upwork: AI copywriting, background removal, or AI voiceovers. Services pay this month; products compound later.
  7. Day 7 — Follow up and improve. Reply to every comment, fix the listing's weakest point, and plan next week's posts. Then repeat — week 2 is where most first sales actually happen.

Copy-paste prompts that start real work

"Act as an expert on [niche]. Give me an outline for a 20-page beginner e-book about [topic], with chapter titles and the 3 key points in each chapter."

"Write 10 Instagram captions for a small [type of business] promoting [product]. Friendly tone, one clear call to action each, no hype words."

"Rewrite this product description so a busy buyer gets the benefit in the first two lines. Keep it honest — no claims I can't prove: [paste description]"

"Give me 15 video hook lines (under 10 words each) for TikToks about [topic] aimed at [audience]. Curiosity-based, no clickbait lies."

"Turn this messy list of ideas into a clean one-page checklist a beginner can follow step by step: [paste ideas]"

Honest bottom line: AI cuts the production time, not the marketing time. Expect 1–4 weeks to a first sale, ~20% marketplace fees, and several listings that flop before one works. That is normal — not a sign to quit.
