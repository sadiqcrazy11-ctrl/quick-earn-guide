Best Side Hustles in November 2026: Ranked by Income, Sorted by How Fast They Pay

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 2, 2026 · 5 min read

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: The best side hustles in November 2026, ranked by reported income, are AI automation agencies, online courses, faceless YouTube, UGC creation, and TikTok Shop affiliate work. The highest-ranked options are not the fastest: services usually pay in weeks, while content channels often take months to earn anything meaningful.

Richtactic refreshed its 138-idea guide on November 2, 2026. Its top five by reported income are AI automation agencies ($5,000–$50,000 a month), online courses ($5,000–$100,000 a month), faceless YouTube ($1,000–$50,000 a month), UGC creation ($1,000–$15,000 a month), and dropshipping ($1,000–$30,000 a month). These are reported ranges, not promises.

The Honest Anchor: What Most People Actually Earn

Bankrate found the typical US side hustler earns a median of about $200 a month, while the average is about $885 a month. A few high earners pull the average up. Plan for a few hundred dollars a month at first.

That gap matters. The ranges above describe what top performers report at scale — not what a beginner banks in month one. The ranking below keeps both views in the same place: income first, then how fast the first dollar really arrives.

1. AI Automation Agency — Highest Income, Fast If You Pitch

Small businesses pay for boring problems solved: missed calls, lead follow-ups, bookings. Startup is $0–$500. First clients land in 2–6 weeks, but only if you pitch.

Build one demo for one business type, pitch 20 similar businesses, and charge setup plus a monthly retainer. One narrow demo you can show beats a long list of services you have never delivered. This is the top-ranked hustle by income because businesses pay for time saved and leads recovered, not for the tool itself.

2. Online Courses — High Ceiling, Slow Build

Online courses rank second by reported income at $5,000–$100,000 a month, but the ceiling hides the build. A course needs an audience or a proven skill first.

Start with a small paid workshop, and expand it only if people buy that. A workshop tests the topic, the price, and your teaching in a week. A full course built before anyone has paid is months of work on a guess.

3. Faceless YouTube — Scalable, Slowest First Dollar

Faceless YouTube can reach $1,000–$50,000 a month in reported ranges, and it scales without filming yourself. It is also the slowest first dollar on this list.

Honest guides put meaningful earnings at 6–18 months of uploads, and most channels never reach full-time income. YouTube blocks monetization for mass-produced AI content, so original scripting is essential. If you start a channel in November, plan for it as a 2027 income stream, not a November one.

4. UGC Creation — The Fastest Content Path

UGC creation is the fastest content path because brands pay for finished videos, not followers. Reported income runs $1,000–$15,000 a month. Beginner rates run $50–$150 per short video, with usage rights charged separately.

First paid work lands in 2–4 weeks. Film three practice videos with products you own, then pitch small brands. Your portfolio is the pitch — three clean, watchable videos matter more than any follower count.

5. TikTok Shop Affiliate and Dropshipping — Fast to Start, Hard to Profit

TikTok Shop affiliate work and dropshipping are fast to start at $0–$100, and hard to profit from. Richtactic lists TikTok Shop affiliate at $100–$30,000 a month, and dropshipping at $1,000–$30,000 a month.

Product choice matters more than posting volume, and fees eat margins fast. Test one product before scaling. A single product you understand, priced with fees included, teaches you more than ten products posted on hope.

Your November Starter Plan: 30 Days, One Hustle

Pick one hustle and give it 30 days.

  1. Week one: build one sample — one demo, one workshop outline, three UGC videos, or one product test.
  2. Week two: pitch or publish daily. The sample only counts once someone sees it.
  3. Week three: follow up and fix the complaint that keeps coming up.
  4. Week four: judge on evidence, and change the niche or offer if nobody paid.

Two rules protect you while you test. First, never pay to start: legitimate clients pay you, and the FTC has shut down AI-income schemes that took at least $25 million from buyers. Second, set 25–30% aside for tax: US self-employment income over $400 a year is taxable.

FAQ

Q: What is the best side hustle in November 2026 for a beginner?
A: A service that pays in weeks beats a channel that pays in months. AI automation for one local business type, or UGC videos for small brands, brings a first client faster than faceless YouTube or a course.

Q: How much does the average side hustler actually make?
A: Bankrate puts the median at about $200 a month and the average at about $885 a month. Most earn a modest supplement at first, not a second salary.

Q: How fast will I earn my first dollar?
A: Services usually pay in 2–6 weeks with steady pitching. UGC takes 2–4 weeks from first pitch to first paid video. Faceless YouTube and courses usually take months, and many never earn much.
