Christmas Seasonal Jobs 2026: Who Is Hiring, Real Pay Rates, and How to Apply

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 12, 2026 · 3 min read

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: Christmas seasonal jobs in 2026 pay roughly $15 to $24 an hour in retail and $17 to $23 an hour for seasonal delivery drivers. Retailers plan about 450,000 seasonal hires — the lowest since 2008 — so apply in November, on the employer's own careers page, and never pay anything to start a job.

Seasonal money runs on a schedule: apply in October and November, earn in November and December. The 2026 season comes with a twist worth knowing upfront. According to Challenger, Gray & Christmas's September 2026 outlook, retailers plan to hire about 450,000 seasonal workers this holiday quarter — down from 461,500 last year, and the lowest seasonal hiring since 2008. Retailers are filling peak demand with automation, existing staff, and on-demand labor instead of big temporary hiring waves. That means fewer traditional seasonal jobs to fall back on, but steadier demand for delivery, warehouse, and gig work.

Who Is Hiring

Amazon has run roughly 250,000 US seasonal hires in each of the last three years. UPS brought on 125,000 seasonal workers in 2024. Spirit Halloween staffed a record 52,000 seasonal associates across 1,575 stores this year. Michaels announced more than 10,000 seasonal team members in September, and says nearly half of last year's seasonal in-store roles converted to regular positions — so a seasonal job can sometimes become permanent if you ask and perform. US Bureau of Labor Statistics data cited in a 2026 roundup found selected retail industries added about 492,000 jobs between October and December.

Christmas Seasonal Jobs 2026: Real Pay Rates

Seasonal postings recently cited starting wages of $15 to $24 per hour at Target, depending on role and market, and many retailers pay at or above the local minimum wage. A recent roundup cited seasonal drivers at UPS at $17 to $23 per hour. Weekend or night shifts sometimes carry a premium — check the exact rate in the advert before accepting, because rates vary widely by employer and location. Gig alternatives pay by the task rather than the hour: one 2026 roundup cited $20 to $33 per hour for gift-wrapping bookings on TaskRabbit during the pre-holiday weeks.

How to Apply — and What to Watch

Apply on the employer's own careers page, never through a random hiring message. Evening and weekend shifts fit best around a main job, and managers want people available on peak dates, including December 23 and 24. Check when payday falls before you count on the money: pay earned in December does not always arrive in time for Christmas shopping, depending on the payroll cycle.

Two scam warnings matter every year. Never pay to start a job — no fee for training, uniforms, or background checks paid by you upfront is normal for these roles. And refuse any cheque you are asked to deposit and send money back from; that is a classic fake-job scam. Seasonal schedules can be unpredictable and December hours are intense, so go in expecting hard weeks, not easy money. All seasonal income is taxable, even small amounts, so keep a simple log of what you earn and from where.

FAQ

Q: When should I apply for Christmas seasonal jobs?
A: In October and November. Managers build rosters early, and the best shifts are usually committed by mid-November. Applying in December means choosing from whatever is left.

Q: How much do Christmas seasonal jobs pay?
A: Roughly $15 to $24 an hour in retail and $17 to $23 an hour for seasonal delivery drivers, depending on the employer, role, and local market. Always check the exact rate in the job advert.

Q: Can a seasonal job become permanent?
A: Sometimes. Michaels says nearly half of last year's seasonal in-store roles converted to regular positions. Treat the seasonal role as a trial: show up reliably, and ask the hiring manager about permanent openings before the season ends.
