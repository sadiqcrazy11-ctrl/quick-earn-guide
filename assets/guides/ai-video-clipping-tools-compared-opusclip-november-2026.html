AI Video Clipping Tools Compared, November 2026: OpusClip $15, Klap $29, and the Views That Pay for Them

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read · Updated on November 27, 2026

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: OpusClip Starter costs $15 a month for 150 minutes of source video; Pro is $29 for 300 minutes. Klap is $29 a month for 100 clips, Vizard starts at $29, and Submagic at $19. Clipping campaigns pay a median of about $1.09 per 1,000 approved views, so a $15 plan needs 15,000 approved views a month to pay for itself.

Clipping was one of only three hustles that survived this morning's audit of 25 viral AI side hustles — the other 22 failed before earning $1. But every “get paid to clip” video sells the tool and hides the math. The tool is a cost. The views are the income. Before you pay for any clipping tool, here is what each one costs, what the work actually pays, and how many views it takes to break even.

The billing unit is the trap. OpusClip and Vizard charge by the length of what you upload — a 60-minute podcast costs 60 credits the moment you hit process, whether the clips are usable or not. Klap and Submagic count what comes out: Klap counts finished clips, Submagic counts finished videos. A weekly hour-long show uses about 240–300 credits a month, which fits one Pro pack and overruns Starter.

What the Work Actually Pays

Content Rewards, the biggest clipping marketplace, counted its own live campaigns on November 10, 2026: a median of about $1.09 per 1,000 approved views across 707 campaigns, with $1.00 the most common rate. A recheck on November 16 found a $1.00 median across 710 campaigns. The latest live count (November 23) showed 741 campaigns paying per 1,000 views, and 638 of them need no application. The platform keeps 10% of each payout, and pay comes in rounds: 7 days of earning plus a 3-day hold. Vyro advertises $3 per 1,000 views, though its listed campaigns mostly show $1–$2.

The Break-Even Math

At the $1.09 median, after the 10% fee you keep about $0.98 per 1,000 approved views. So a $15 Starter plan needs about 15,300 approved views a month to cover itself (13,800 before the fee), and a $29 Pro plan needs about 29,600 (26,600 before the fee). At the $1.00 median instead, $15 needs about 16,700 views and $29 needs about 32,200. One clip at 20,000 approved views earns about $21.80 gross, $19.60 after the fee — roughly one Starter month. A clip with no views earns nothing, and pay is per approved view, not per hour. The tool does not create the views; it only cuts the clip.

Which Tool for Which Person

  • Clipping someone else's streams for campaigns: start free. OpusClip's free plan (60 credits, watermarked) or Vizard's free plan (60 credits, watermarked, 720p) cost nothing — pay only after a clip actually earns.
  • A weekly long podcast or stream: OpusClip Pro ($29, 300 credits) or Klap Basic ($29, 100 clips); Klap wins when the source is very long, because it counts clips, not minutes.
  • Polishing finished clips: Submagic Starter ($19) is a captions-first finishing tool, not a clip finder — it fits creators who already have their clips.

The Honest Catches

  • Credits are charged on upload, not on results. A long video costs credits the moment you process it, even if none of the clips are usable.
  • Free exports expire after 3 days on OpusClip, and free plans carry a watermark.
  • Rates are advertised medians, not guaranteed payouts. Individual campaigns pay $0.80–$2.00 per 1,000 views, and campaigns can fill up or end.
  • Views, not the tool, decide whether you get paid. That is the gate in every clipping guide on this site.

Bottom Line

OpusClip Starter at $15 is the cheapest honest starting point for clipping, and Klap at $29 is the better buy if your source videos run long. But neither one pays for itself until your clips clear roughly 15,000–30,000 approved views a month. Start on a free plan, prove a clip can earn, and only then pay for the tool.

FAQ

Q: How much does OpusClip cost in November 2026?
A: Free is $0 for 60 credits a month with a watermark. Starter is $15 a month for 150 credits. Pro is $29 a month per 300-credit pack, or $14.50 a month billed yearly. One credit equals one minute of source video.

Q: How much do clipping campaigns pay per 1,000 views?
A: Content Rewards counted a median of about $1.09 per 1,000 approved views across 707 live campaigns on November 10, 2026, and $1.00 on a November 16 recheck. The most common rate is exactly $1.00, and the platform keeps 10% of each payout.

Q: How many views does a $15 clipping plan need to pay for itself?
A: About 15,300 approved views in a month at the $1.09 median after the 10% fee (13,800 before the fee). At the $1.00 median it is about 16,700. One 20,000-view clip earns about $19.60 after the fee — just over a Starter month.
