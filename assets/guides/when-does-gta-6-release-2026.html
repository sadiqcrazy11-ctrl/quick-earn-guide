When Does GTA 6 Release? Launch Date, Platforms, PC, and How to Earn From It

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 22, 2026 · 3 min read · Updated on November 22, 2026

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: GTA 6 is out. It launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, priced at $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. There is no PC version announced, and the launch game is single-player only — no online mode. The honest way to earn from the launch is content around the game: clipping, guides, and your own channel. The game itself pays you nothing.

The question "when does gta 6 release" was the most searched gaming question of the last three years, and now it has an answer: it already did. After two delays — first from fall 2025 to May 26, 2026, then to November 19, 2026 — Rockstar’s game went gold and launched on schedule. Pre-loading opened on November 10, and Sensor Tower estimated about 24.5 million copies sold across pre-orders and day one — a third-party estimate, not Rockstar’s own number. The weeks after release are when millions of players are still searching for answers, and that search traffic is where the earning is.

What launched — and what did not

GTA 6 launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. The Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition $99.99, the same on both consoles. The Ultimate Edition adds premium vehicles, weapons, apparel, and an Ultimate Edition Upgrade that Standard owners can buy separately for $20. Physical copies contain a download code instead of a disc. Pre-orders and purchases before November 20, 2026 included the Vintage Vice City Pack plus a free month of GTA+. Rockstar says to clear up to 212GB before pre-load. What did not launch: a PC version (Rockstar has not announced one at all), and any online mode — the launch game is a single-player experience, full stop.

Tom Henderson’s predictions: how long is GTA 6?

Insider Gaming’s Tom Henderson reported on November 15, 2026 that the campaign runs about 50 hours across nearly 100 missions in five chapters, and that earning the PS5 Platinum trophy could take around 200 hours, including scanning 170 wildlife species, with some trophies tied to random chance. These are pre-release reports from people with knowledge of the game — Rockstar has not confirmed the campaign length, the mission count, or the trophy requirements. Treat them as informed estimates, not official numbers. For creators they still matter: completion and collectible guides are the highest-value written content in the weeks after launch, because that is exactly what stuck players search for.

How to earn from the launch

First, clipping campaigns. Marketplaces like Whop Content Rewards pay per 1,000 verified views on short vertical clips cut from streams — reported rates run $0.20 to $6 per 1,000 views, with a median around $1. Free tools like CapCut and DaVinci Resolve cover the editing. Only clip streams you have permission to use, never use leaked footage, and avoid big spoilers. Second, your own channel. YouTube Shorts pay once you reach 1,000 subscribers and 10 million Shorts views in 90 days (20 million for new applicants from February 1, 2027). Launch-week search traffic is the opportunity, but the payout comes months later. Third, written guides. This is the untapped-query play from the video that started this article: find the exact questions people type — "when does gta 6 release", "will gta 6 have online", "is gta cross platform" — answer them honestly and completely, and earn from AdSense on the organic traffic. Fourth, what not to do: never pay to join a clipping campaign (real campaigns pay you), never promise viewers a fixed income, and never sell "GTA 6 Online" access — it does not exist.

The honest timeline

Launch-week clipping can pay within weeks if a clip takes off, but most clips earn little. A channel takes months to reach monetization. A guide article takes days to write and months to rank. None of this is fast money. If someone promises you a fixed income from GTA 6 content, they are selling you something.

FAQ

Q: When does GTA 6 release?
A: It already did — November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The date survived two delays and a "gone gold" confirmation from Take-Two.

Q: Will GTA 6 come to PC?
A: Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a date. Treat any PC release date you see as unconfirmed.

Q: How long is GTA 6?
A: Insider Gaming’s Tom Henderson reported about 50 hours for the story and about 200 hours for full completion. Rockstar has not confirmed either number.

Q: Can you make money from GTA 6 content?
A: Yes — clipping campaigns, your own channel, and written guides. Rates are per-view and vary; nothing is guaranteed, and you need permission to clip other people’s streams.
