Buy Now or Wait for Black Friday 2026? The Verdict on Every Big Deal

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 27, 2026 · 5 min read · Updated on November 27, 2026

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: For the big confirmed prices — AirPods Pro 3 at $149, AirTag 2 at $18, Apple Watch Series 12 from $299, MacBook Neo from $599 — buy now. CNN Underscored reports most of these will not get better next week or on Cyber Monday, and the real risk is stock, not price. The only thing worth waiting for is an item that has not dropped yet: those land in Amazon's double drops at midnight and 8 a.m. PT on November 27 and November 30.

Every year the same question costs people money: buy now, or wait for Black Friday? This year the answer is unusually clear, because the deepest prices arrived a week early and then stayed put. Here is the verdict on every big deal, item by item, based on prices confirmed on November 27, 2026.

Buy Now: The Verdicts

  • AirPods Pro 3 — $149 (was $249): BUY. The lowest price ever tracked, and they already sold out once at this price, on November 24. Waiting risks an empty listing, not a better price.
  • AirTag 2 (single) — $18 (was $29): BUY. An all-time low, $5 below the previous best. The 4-pack is $79 if you need more than one.
  • Apple Watch Series 12 — from $299 (was $399): BUY. All-time low on both sizes; the 46mm is $349 (was $449).
  • MacBook Neo — from $599 (was $700): BUY. Down another $50 this week, and $100 off is the deepest cut on it so far.
  • M5 MacBook Air — from $989 (13-inch): BUY. Up to $400 off across the lineup, and the first time the 13-inch has gone under $1,000.
  • Echo Dot Max — nearly 60% off: BUY. The deepest cut on an Amazon device this week.
  • AirPods Max 2 — $419 (was $549): BUY. $130 off, and the price has held all week.

CNN Underscored's line, repeated all week: most of these prices "won't get better next week or even into Cyber Monday." These are the Black Friday prices, not a warm-up.

Wait: What Has Not Dropped Yet

The only thing worth waiting for is an item that has not dropped at all. Those land in Amazon's double drops — midnight and 8 a.m. PT today, November 27, and again on Monday, November 30. Walmart's Cyber Monday sale runs online only from November 30 through December 2. If the thing you want is not on sale anywhere yet, set the alarm for those drops instead of buying a substitute today.

The Two Rules That Decide It

  • A live price is only live until it sells out. The $149 AirPods sold out on November 24 and came back November 25. A price you saw this morning is not a promise for tonight.
  • Walmart gives no price adjustments. If you buy at Walmart and the price drops later, the difference stays with Walmart. Buy only confirmed lows there, and buy them once.

The Apple Gift Card Trap

Apple's own Black Friday event (November 27–30) gives gift cards, not discounts: a $50 card with AirPods Pro 3 at the full $249 price, up to $300 on bigger purchases. On anything Amazon has on sale, the cash cut wins — $100 off beats a $50 card. Apple's event only makes sense for products nobody else has discounted.

Bottom Line

Buy the confirmed lows today: AirPods Pro 3 at $149, AirTag 2 at $18, Watch Series 12 from $299, MacBook Neo from $599, M5 MacBook Air from $989. Wait only for things that have not dropped yet, and catch them in the midnight and 8 a.m. PT drops on November 27 and November 30. Waiting for a deeper cut on a price that is already an all-time low is how people end up paying full price in December.

FAQ

Q: Should I buy AirPods Pro 3 now or wait for Cyber Monday?
A: Buy now. They are $149 at Amazon and Walmart as of November 27, 2026 — $100 off and the lowest price ever tracked — and they already sold out once at this price. CNN Underscored reports the price will not get better on Cyber Monday; the risk is that it sells out before Monday.

Q: What if the price drops after I buy?
A: On Amazon, you can usually cancel an unshipped order and rebuy — but the price is unlikely to drop. On Walmart, there are no price adjustments at all, so a buy-now price that drops later stays dropped. Buy only confirmed lows, and buy them once.

Q: Is it better to buy in store today or online?
A: Online, for the big prices — the $149 AirPods and $18 AirTag are the same price online and in store, and the online stock is easier to check. The store is only worth it for the doorbusters and gift-card lines: Target is giving gift cards to the first 100 guests, and Best Buy has a $100 gift card for $60 for members.
