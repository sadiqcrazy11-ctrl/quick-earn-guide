Guide 14 · $0 Capital

$0 Capital Emergency Cash Guide

By Sadiq Khan · Published October 2026 · 2 min read

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: With zero money, your assets are time, a phone, and things other people are giving away. Flip free items, run local service arbitrage, and take micro-freelancing tasks. Local cash gigs can pay the same day; platform payouts are slower and may require ID at withdrawal.

  • Flip free items. Check the free sections of Facebook Marketplace and Craigslist daily. Clean the item, take bright photos, relist it honestly. Small flips ($10–$50) add up and cost nothing but time.
  • Local service arbitrage. Find a client who needs lawn care, moving help, or cleaning; agree a price; hire reliable help for less; keep the margin and manage the job. You sell coordination, not labor.
  • Micro-freelancing tasks. Data entry, transcription, background removal, and captioning gigs on Fiverr/Upwork need no upfront money — just a phone or laptop and fast, careful work.
  • Sell what's already in your home. Unused clothes, cables, games, and furniture are day-one inventory. Photograph them today; list them tonight.
Honest bottom line: $0-capital hustles trade time for money — that is the deal, and it works. Cash gigs pay same-day; money earned on platforms may sit until you pass ID verification at withdrawal. Never pay a “startup fee” to anyone who promises you work — that is the scam version of this guide.
