Amazon Black Friday Week 2026: November 19-30 Dates, Double Drops and What to Buy

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 12, 2026 · 6 min read · Updated on November 21, 2026

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: Amazon Black Friday Week 2026 runs November 19 to 30, starting at 12:01 a.m. PST on November 19 and ending on Cyber Monday, November 30. Amazon announced the dates on November 9, 2026. Its daily Today's Big Deals feature savings of up to 60% off for the first time, with double drops at midnight and 8 a.m. PST on both Black Friday (November 27) and Cyber Monday (November 30). Deals on Amazon's own devices started earlier, from November 16. Best confirmed prices checked November 18: Fire TV Stick 4K (2026) $19.99 (was $60), AirPods Pro 3 $179.99 (was $249), AirTag 2 $17.50. No Prime membership is needed for the main deals.

Amazon spent weeks letting deal sites guess its Black Friday dates. On November 9, it finally announced them: Black Friday Week and Cyber Monday run November 19 to 30, 2026 — 12 days of deals, with the first eight days landing before Thanksgiving. The event is live now, and this guide lists only prices actually checked, no forecasts.

Amazon Black Friday Week 2026: The Dates That Matter

November 16: Amazon's own-device deals started — Echo, Fire TV Stick, Kindle, Ring, and Blink. Alexa tablets followed on November 18. These are the earliest confirmed discounts in the whole event.

November 19, 12:01 a.m. PST: Black Friday Week opened. Millions of deals went live across electronics, beauty, fashion, toys, and home, from brands including Le Creuset, HP, Google, and Stanley. New themed deal drops arrive every day.

November 27 (Black Friday): The biggest single day. Today's Big Deals drop twice — at midnight and 8 a.m. PST — with savings up to 60% off for the first time.

November 30 (Cyber Monday): The event ends the same way it peaked: another double drop at midnight and 8 a.m. PST, up to 60% off.

Your Day-by-Day Plan

  1. November 19 (opening day): Grab the confirmed all-time lows first — the $19.99 Fire TV Stick and $179.99 AirPods Pro 3. Cheap deals sell out within hours.
  2. November 20-26: Check the daily themed drops each morning (live 3:01 a.m. Eastern) for mid-tier deals — TVs, home goods, toys.
  3. November 27 and 30: Set an alarm for midnight and 8 a.m. Pacific — the double drops bring the biggest deals, savings up to 60% for the first time.

How to Tell a Real Deal From a Label

  • Check the price on the buy button, not the headline. Amazon names percentages — the number at checkout is the one that matters.
  • Tap the Price History button first. It shows up to 365 days of pricing, and a "deal" that was cheaper last month is not a deal.
  • Prices change fast. A price checked this morning may be gone by tonight.

Buy Now or Wait?

Buy now if you need an Amazon device. The Echo, Fire TV Stick, Kindle, Ring, and Blink deals started November 16, and these are the products Amazon discounts hardest every year. If the early price is already at last year's low, waiting rarely beats it.

Wait if you want the headline brands. The deepest cuts land on Black Friday (November 27) and Cyber Monday (November 30), when Today's Big Deals drop twice a day. Make your list now, check the price history on anything over $100, and remember that a discount only saves money if you were already going to buy the thing.

The Honest Catches

  • Early is not always cheapest. The $19.99 Fire TV Stick is a genuine all-time low, but other "early deals" are just the regular price with a Black Friday label.
  • No Prime gate, but Prime still matters. Some of the best extras are Prime-only, though the main deals are open to everyone.
  • Stock runs out. The deepest discounts are limited — if a deal matters, buy it when you see it.

Three Rules for the Rest of the Event

  1. Set two alarms for the double drops. Midnight and 8 a.m. PST on November 27 and November 30 are when the up-to-60% deals appear, and the best ones sell out fast.
  2. Check the Holiday Shop early. Early deals are live now — if the item you want is already at a confirmed low, you do not have to wait for the event.
  3. Ignore the forecasts. Seller sites disagreed for weeks about the start date (November 14, 19, 20, and 26 were all claimed). The only dates that matter are the ones Amazon announced: November 19 to 30.

FAQs

When does Amazon Black Friday Week 2026 start and end?

November 19, 2026, at 12:01 a.m. PST, running through Cyber Monday, November 30. Amazon announced the dates on November 9, 2026.

When are the biggest deals?

Black Friday (November 27) and Cyber Monday (November 30), when Today's Big Deals drop twice — at midnight and 8 a.m. PST — with savings up to 60% off.

What is the best Amazon Black Friday deal live now?

The Fire TV Stick 4K (2026) at $19.99, down from $60 — an all-time low, checked November 18. AirPods Pro 3 at $179.99 (was $249) and Dyson V9 at $269.95 (was $599.99) are the other standouts.

Do I need Prime to shop Amazon Black Friday Week?

No. The main Black Friday Week deals are open to all shoppers. Some offers are reserved for Prime members, and a free 30-day Prime trial is available if you are eligible.
