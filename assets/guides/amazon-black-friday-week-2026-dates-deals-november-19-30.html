Amazon Black Friday Week 2026: November 19-30 Dates, Double Drops and What to Buy

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 12, 2026 · 5 min read

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: Amazon Black Friday Week 2026 runs November 19 to 30, starting at 12:01 a.m. PST on November 19 and ending on Cyber Monday, November 30. Amazon announced the dates on November 9, 2026. Today's Big Deals go up to 60% off for the first time, with double drops at midnight and 8 a.m. PST on Black Friday (November 27) and Cyber Monday (November 30). Deals on Amazon's own devices start earlier, on November 16.

Amazon is running its Black Friday sale as a twelve-day event this year, not a single day. If you only remember two moments, make them the double drops: the deepest discounts land twice on November 27 and twice again on November 30.

Amazon Black Friday Week 2026: The Key Dates

November 16 — Amazon's own devices go first. Deals on Echo, Fire TV Stick, Kindle, Ring and Blink start here, three days before the main event. Alexa tablets follow on November 18.

November 19, 12:01 a.m. PST — Black Friday Week opens. Millions of deals go live, including Le Creuset, HP, Google and Stanley, with daily themed drops running through the event.

November 27 — Black Friday double drop. New Today's Big Deals land at midnight and again at 8 a.m. PST, up to 60% off.

November 30 — Cyber Monday double drop. The same midnight and 8 a.m. PST pattern closes the event.

One honest note: Amazon has not published its full item-level price list yet, so exact prices on most products are still unconfirmed.

Confirmed Discounts

These are the discount levels Amazon has confirmed for the event:

  • Up to 50% off select Levi's fashion
  • Up to 40% off medicube and Color Wow beauty
  • Up to 40% off LEGO and Hasbro toys
  • Up to 35% off Samsung, Bose and Anker electronics
  • Over $200 off a Breville espresso machine
  • Over $300 off a eufy robot vacuum and a NordicTrack treadmill

Early deals are already live in Amazon's Holiday Shop. Deal sites also report AirPods Pro 3 at $199 (checked November 4) — if that price is still showing and AirPods were already on your list, you do not have to wait for November 19.

Buy Now or Wait?

Buy from November 16: Amazon's own devices. Echo, Fire TV Stick, Kindle, Ring and Blink are cheapest during Amazon's own events, and their prices rarely drop further on Black Friday itself.

Wait for November 27 and 30: the headline brands. Levi's, LEGO, Samsung, Bose and the big-ticket items get their deepest cuts in the double drops, not on opening day.

Either way, make a list before the sale starts and check the price history on anything over $100. A discount only saves you money if you were already going to buy the thing — a 40% saving on something you did not need is still 60% spent.

Three Rules for Black Friday Week

  1. Set two alarms. The double drops land at midnight and 8 a.m. PST on November 27 and November 30. The best Today's Big Deals sell out fast, so be there when they drop, not hours later.
  2. Check the Holiday Shop early. Real deals are already live there now. If something on your list is already at your target price, buy it and stop watching the sale.
  3. Ignore seller-site forecasts. Different seller sites claimed the sale would start November 14, 19, 20 and 26. Only Amazon's announced dates matter: the event runs November 19 to 30.

FAQ

Q: When does Amazon Black Friday Week 2026 start?
A: November 19, 2026, at 12:01 a.m. PST, running through Cyber Monday, November 30.

Q: When are the biggest deals?
A: On Black Friday, November 27, and Cyber Monday, November 30, when double drops at midnight and 8 a.m. PST push Today's Big Deals up to 60% off.

Q: Do I need Prime?
A: No. The main deals are open to all shoppers. Some offers are Prime-only, and a free 30-day trial is available if you are eligible.
