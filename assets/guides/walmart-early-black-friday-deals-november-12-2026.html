Walmart Early Black Friday Deals November 12, 2026: Three Waves, One Honest Plan

By Sadiq Khan · Published November 7, 2026 · 4 min read

Share:WhatsAppTelegramX

Quick Answer: Walmart early Black Friday deals run November 12 to 15, 2026, online from 12:01 a.m. ET November 12 and in stores from 6 a.m. local time. Walmart+ members get first crack at limited hot Deal drops on November 12; leftovers open to everyone on November 13. Two more waves follow: Black Friday November 23 to 29, and Cyber Monday November 30 to December 2.

Walmart is the last big retailer to open its early Black Friday sale, and it is doing it in three waves instead of one big day. Its first wave starts Thursday, November 12.

Walmart Early Black Friday Deals: The Three Waves

Wave 1 — Early Black Friday Deals, November 12 to 15. Online from 12:01 a.m. ET November 12, in stores from 6 a.m. local time. Walmart+ members get early access to hot Deal drops on November 12; leftovers open to all shoppers on November 13.

Wave 2 — Black Friday Deals, November 23 to 29. The main event. Online from 12:01 a.m. ET November 23, in stores from 6 a.m. on Black Friday, November 27. Walmart+ members get the same one-day head start.

Wave 3 — Cyber Monday, November 30 to December 2. Online only, from 12:01 a.m. ET November 30, with a three-hour early window for Walmart+ members.

The full Black Friday ad is not out yet, so November 12 item prices are still unconfirmed.

Deals Already Live

Checked November 6 on Walmart.com: AirPods 4 for $89, AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation for $119.97, and Beats Studio Pro for $149. These are live now, not November 12 doorbusters — if you want them, you do not have to wait.

Buy Now or Wait?

Buy now only if the price is a confirmed all-time low on something you were already going to buy. The deepest discounts still land November 23 to 30.

Worth buying now: the AirPods 4 at $89 and the Beats Studio Pro at $149, if those were already on your list. Both are strong prices, and popular tech sells out before the main event.

Worth waiting on: everything in the unreleased ad. Walmart has not published its November 12 item prices yet, so any "deal" you see before then is a guess. And here is the catch that matters most: Walmart.com offers no price adjustments. If you buy now and the price drops on November 23, you do not get the difference back.

Three Rules Before November 12

  1. Make your list now. Decide what you actually need before the ad drops, so a flashy discount cannot invent a purchase.
  2. Check the price history first. A "was $129.99, now $99" tag only means something if $129.99 was the real recent price.
  3. Remember the no-adjustment rule. If you are not certain the price is the lowest it will go, wait for November 23 to 29. A missed deal beats an overpaid one.

FAQ

Q: When does Walmart's Early Black Friday sale start?
A: November 12, 2026, at 12:01 a.m. ET online and 6 a.m. local time in stores, running through November 15.

Q: Do I need Walmart+ to shop the deals?
A: No. Members get a one-day head start on limited hot Deal drops, but most deals are open to all shoppers from the start.

Q: Does Walmart match prices if a deal drops later?
A: No. Walmart.com offers no price adjustments — a buy-now price that drops later stays dropped.
