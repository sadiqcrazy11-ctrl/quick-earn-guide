About this tool

What this TikTok Shop fee calculator does

TikTok Shop charges sellers a referral fee — 6% on most categories — and unlike some marketplaces, that fee already includes payment processing. There is no second transaction fee, no listing fee, and no monthly fee. This calculator applies the right rate for your category, handles the 3% new-seller window and the collectibles split above $10,000, and subtracts your item and shipping costs to show true profit.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $30 sale in a standard category, a $10 item, $5 to ship. The referral fee is $1.80, you receive $28.20, and after your $15 of costs you keep $13.20 — a 44% margin on the sale price. As a new seller at 3%, the fee drops to $0.90 and profit rises to $14.10.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter the sale price and pick your category — most products are 6%.
  2. If you are inside your first 30 days after your first sale, switch on the 3% new-seller rate.
  3. Add your item cost and real shipping cost to see true profit, not just the fee.

Who it is for

TikTok Shop sellers pricing products, affiliates checking what sellers keep, and anyone comparing TikTok Shop against Etsy or Amazon.

Honest limitations

Affiliate commissions (often 5–20%) are separate and not modeled — they come out of the same margin. Refund admin fees, advertising, and Shop Plus effects are excluded. Some blogs quote 8%; TikTok's own seller pages say 6% for most categories, and this calculator follows the official figure. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

TikTok Shop Fee Calculator — common questions

What is TikTok Shop's fee in 2026?

A 6% referral fee on most US categories, 5% on fine jewelry and pre-owned, and 3% on the portion above $10,000 for collectibles and pre-owned. New sellers pay 3% for 30 days after their first sale.

Is there a separate payment processing fee on TikTok Shop?

No. The referral fee already includes payment processing, so there is no second transaction fee on top of the 6%.

What is the TikTok Shop new-seller rate?

New sellers pay a 3% referral fee for 30 days after their first sale, instead of 6%. After the window ends, the standard category rate applies.

What happens to the fee if a buyer returns the item?

TikTok Shop charges a refund administration fee of 20% of the referral fee, capped at $5 per SKU. The referral fee itself is generally refunded on the returned order.
