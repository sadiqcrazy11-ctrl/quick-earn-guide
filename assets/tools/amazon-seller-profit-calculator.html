About this tool

What this Amazon seller profit calculator does

Amazon charges a referral fee on the total amount the buyer pays — price plus any shipping you charge — and the percentage depends on your category: 15% for most of the catalog, 8% for electronics and computers, tiered rates for clothing, beauty, and grocery, and 45% for Amazon device accessories. On top of that sit fulfillment (FBA fees by size and weight, plus a 3.5% surcharge since April 2026), your selling plan ($39.99 a month Professional, or $0.99 per item Individual), and your own product and inbound shipping costs. This calculator stacks all of it into one per-unit profit figure, a margin, and the breakeven units that cover your monthly fixed costs.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $29.99 item in a 15% category, $3.50 fulfillment, $8 product cost, $0.50 inbound shipping, Professional plan at 100 units a month. The referral fee is $4.50, the plan costs $0.40 per unit, total cost per unit is $16.90, and profit is $13.09 — a 43.7% margin. Breakeven is just 4 units a month against the $39.99 plan. Drop the price to $14.99 and the same stack leaves $5.84 — the fixed costs do not shrink with the price.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Pick your category. Tiered categories (clothing, beauty, grocery, furniture) apply their published breakpoints automatically; pick Custom to enter any rate.
  2. Enter your price, fulfillment cost per unit, product cost, and inbound shipping. For FBA, use the exact fee from Seller Central's revenue calculator for your size tier.
  3. Set your plan and expected monthly units — the calculator spreads the $39.99 plan across them and shows breakeven against all fixed costs.

Who it is for

New sellers validating a product before ordering inventory, existing sellers repricing, and anyone comparing FBA against shipping orders themselves (FBM).

Honest limitations

Storage fees, long-term storage surcharges, advertising (PPC), returns processing, and referral-fee promotions are not included — real margins are thinner than this page shows once ads start. Category assignment follows Amazon's browse node, which can change. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Amazon Seller Profit Calculator — common questions

What is Amazon's referral fee in 2026?

15% of the total sale price in most categories, with a $0.30 per-unit minimum. Electronics and computers pay 8%, clothing is tiered (5% to $15, 10% to $20, 17% above), beauty and baby pay 8% to $10 then 15%, and Amazon device accessories pay 45%. Rates were frozen for 2025–2026.

What does FBA cost per unit?

FBA fulfillment fees start at $2.43 per unit for the smallest size tier and rise with size and weight, plus a 3.5% fuel and logistics surcharge since April 2026. Your exact fee depends on your product's size tier — check it in Seller Central's revenue calculator and enter it here.

Should I choose the Professional or Individual plan?

Professional costs $39.99 a month with no per-item fee; Individual costs $0.99 per item sold. At 40+ units a month, Professional is cheaper on plan cost alone — and it unlocks reports and promotions Individual sellers cannot use. This calculator shows the plan cost per unit at your volume.

Why is my margin lower than this calculator shows?

Most often advertising. PPC spend, storage fees, and returns are real costs this page does not model. Add your average ad spend per unit to the fulfillment field for a closer number.
