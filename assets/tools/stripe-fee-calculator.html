About this tool

What this Stripe fee calculator does

A Stripe fee calculator applies Stripe's standard US pricing to a card payment: 2.9% of the charge plus a fixed 30 cents, charged only when the payment succeeds. Surcharges stack on top: 1.5% for a card issued outside the US, 1% when the payment must be converted into another currency, and 0.5% when the card number is typed in by hand. Reverse mode solves backwards: charge = (amount you want + fixed fee) ÷ (1 − rate).

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $100 domestic online charge costs $3.20 (2.9% is $2.90, plus $0.30), so you keep $96.80. Add the international and conversion surcharges and the rate becomes 5.4% + $0.30: the fee is $5.70 and you keep $94.30. Flip to reverse mode and ask to keep $100, and the calculator says charge $103.30.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Pick a mode: what you keep from a charge, or what to charge to keep a target.
  2. Enter the amount and tick any surcharges that apply to the payment.
  3. Edit the rate fields if your Stripe account has custom pricing.

Who it is for

Sellers, freelancers, and small businesses pricing Stripe checkout, Payment Links, or invoices.

Honest limitations

Rates differ by country — this page uses US standard pricing. High-volume accounts can negotiate custom rates. Refunds return the payment but Stripe keeps the original processing fee, and disputes cost $15 each in the US. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Stripe Fee Calculator — common questions

What is Stripe's fee in the US?

Stripe's standard US rate for a successful online card payment is 2.9% plus 30 cents per charge. There is no setup fee or monthly fee on standard pricing. On a $100 charge the fee is $3.20 and you keep $96.80.

What does Stripe charge for international cards?

Stripe adds 1.5% for a card issued outside the US, and another 1% if currency conversion is required, on top of the standard 2.9% plus 30 cents. A $100 international payment with conversion costs $5.70 in fees.

How do I charge enough to receive an exact amount after Stripe fees?

Use reverse mode: charge equals the amount you want plus the fixed fee, divided by one minus the rate. To keep $100 at 2.9% plus $0.30, charge ($100 + $0.30) divided by 0.971, which is $103.30.

Does Stripe charge a fee on refunds?

Stripe does not charge a separate refund fee, but it keeps the original processing fee from the charge. If you refund a $100 payment, you lose the $3.20 fee as well as the $100. Disputes cost $15 each in the US, win or lose.
