About this tool

What this side hustle time calculator does

Most income advice assumes you have unlimited time. This side hustle time calculator starts from the other end: the hours you actually have. Enter your free hours per week, a realistic hourly payout, and the weeks per month you will genuinely work, and it shows your monthly and yearly income. It was built for students first — because for a student, the honest question is never "how much can I earn?" but "what are six hours a week worth without wrecking my grades?"

A quick example

The defaults are the classic student scenario: 8 free hours a week at $15 an hour, four weeks a month. That is $480 a month and $5,760 a year — real money for textbooks, a laptop, or a first emergency fund, earned without touching class time. Try your own schedule honestly: if you only have 5 hours, the calculator will show you about $300 a month instead, and you can plan around the truth.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter the free hours you have each week. For most students, five to ten is the realistic range — not twenty.
  2. Enter a realistic hourly payout for the kind of work you would actually do, from micro-tasks to beginner freelancing.
  3. Set the weeks per month you will really work (four is a fair default), then read your monthly and yearly income.

Who it is for

Students first — but also parents, shift workers, and anyone with a full-time job testing whether a few weekly hours are worth giving up. It helps set expectations before you start: if your hours at a realistic rate produce $120 a month, you can decide with open eyes whether that is worth it, instead of chasing a fantasy number.

Honest limitations

The math assumes you fill every free hour with paid work, which nobody does — finding the work, admin, and plain rest all eat into it. School and your main job come first; a side hustle that costs you your grades or your health is a bad trade at any rate. Treat the result as a best case for a consistent month. FAQ

Side Hustle Time Calculator — common questions

How much can a student earn from a side hustle around school?

At $15/hour, 8 hours a week for 4 weeks a month comes to about $480/month or $5,760/year. The Time-Commitment Calculator lets you plug in your own hours and rate. School comes first — 5–10 hours a week of micro-tasks or freelancing is a realistic start. Estimates only — not financial advice.

Why does the calculator ask for weeks per month?

Because not every month has four full working weeks — holidays, exams, and days off happen. Four is a fair default; use three when you know a month will be interrupted and you want the cautious estimate.

What hourly rate should I enter?

Use the rate for work you could actually get: local part-time wages, micro-task averages, or beginner freelance rates in your skill. If you are unsure, run the calculator at $10, $15, and $25 and compare the monthly outcomes.

Can I really make $500 a month around school?

The math says close: $15/hour for about 8 hours a week across 4 weeks gives $480. Hitting it month after month needs steady clients, which takes time to build — and school still comes first.
