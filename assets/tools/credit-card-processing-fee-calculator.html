The fixed fee is what punishes small sales

On a $10 sale the fixed fee dominates. Use that:

  1. On a $100 sale Stripe online costs $3.20; on a $10 sale it costs $0.59 — 5.9% of the sale.
  2. High-volume sellers can save with interchange-plus pricing, where the true wholesale cost runs about 1.79% + $0.08 in person.
  3. Interchange — paid to the cardholder’s bank, about 2% on average in the US — is the biggest piece of every flat rate, and it is not negotiable.

About this tool

What this credit card processing fee calculator does

Every flat-rate processor bundles three costs into one quote: interchange (paid to the cardholder’s bank, the biggest piece, about 2% on average in the US), the card network’s scheme fee (about 0.13–0.14%), and the processor’s own markup — the only part the processor controls. This calculator applies the published flat rate of the processor you pick to your monthly volume, splits the percentage part from the fixed per-transaction part, and shows the effective rate.

A quick example

$5,000 of monthly sales across 200 transactions on Stripe online: 2.9% is $145, plus 200 × $0.30 = $60, so total fees are $205 — an effective rate of 4.1%. The same volume on Square Free in person costs $130 + $30 = $160.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your monthly card sales and how many transactions that is.
  2. Pick a processor and method — online and in-person rates differ.
  3. Compare the monthly cost and effective rate across processors before you switch.

Who it is for

Small businesses, sellers and freelancers choosing between Stripe, Square and PayPal — or checking whether a paid plan earns its monthly fee.

Honest limitations

International cards add about 1.5% on Stripe and PayPal, plus about 1% for currency conversion on Stripe. Square’s Plus and Premium monthly fees are per location. Chargebacks cost $15 on Stripe. The scheme-fee figure is an indicative range, not a published Visa/Mastercard schedule. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Credit Card Processing Fee Calculator — common questions

What is the average credit card processing fee?

For flat-rate processors in the US, roughly 2.6–3.5% plus $0.09–$0.49 per transaction, depending on processor and method. Underneath, interchange averages about 2% and the scheme fee about 0.13–0.14% — the rest is the processor’s markup.

Is Stripe or Square cheaper?

It depends on how you sell. In person, Square Free (2.6% + $0.15) beats Stripe online (2.9% + $0.30); online, Stripe beats Square Free online (3.3% + $0.30). Run your own volume through both — the fixed fee punishes small tickets.

Why is PayPal 2.99% + $0.49, not 2.9% + $0.30?

PayPal raised its rates: the old 2.9% + $0.30 figure is outdated. Standard PayPal Checkout on cards is now 2.99% + $0.49, and 3.49% + $0.49 when the buyer pays with a PayPal or Venmo balance.

What is interchange-plus pricing?

Instead of one flat rate, you pay the true interchange cost plus the scheme fee plus a fixed processor markup. High-volume sellers often save, because flat rates sit above wholesale cost — in-person interchange-plus averages about 1.79% + $0.08.
