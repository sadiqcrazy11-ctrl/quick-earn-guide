About this tool

What this Etsy fees by country calculator does

Etsy’s transaction fee — 6.5% of the item price plus shipping plus gift wrap — is identical in every country. What changes is the rest. Nine countries pay an extra regulatory operating fee, from 1.97% in Hungary down to 0.05% in India, to cover local regulations and digital services taxes. Payment processing also varies: 3% in the US, 4% in the UK and eurozone, 5% in India. This calculator combines the three percentage fees for your country.

A quick example

A $100 order from France: regulatory fee 1.14% = $1.14, transaction fee 6.5% = $6.50, processing 4% = $4.00. Total percentage fees $11.64, so you keep $88.36 before the $0.20 listing fee and the small fixed processing fee. The same order from the US costs $9.50 in percentage fees.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Pick your seller country — that sets the regulatory operating fee.
  2. Enter the order total (item + shipping + gift wrap).
  3. Pick your payment processing region — that sets the processing %.

Who it is for

Etsy sellers outside the US, and anyone comparing what Etsy takes in different countries before opening a shop.

Honest limitations

The fixed per-order processing fee (e.g. $0.25 US, £0.20 UK, €0.30 eurozone) is not included — the totals here are the percentage parts only, plus the $0.20 listing fee is separate. The regulatory fee is subject to VAT where applicable. Canada and Australia pay 4% processing on international orders. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Etsy Fees by Country Calculator — common questions

Which countries pay Etsy’s regulatory operating fee?

Nine: Hungary (1.97%), Türkiye (1.67%), Vietnam (1.24%), France (1.14%), Spain (0.88%), Italy (0.80%), Canada (0.50%), the United Kingdom (0.48%) and India (0.05%). The United States, Germany, Australia and every other country pay nothing.

What is the regulatory operating fee for?

Etsy says it covers the increased cost of doing business where new regulations apply — local laws, taxes and compliance costs. It is not optional and cannot be opted out of.

Is the Etsy transaction fee the same in every country?

Yes — 6.5% of the item price plus shipping plus gift wrap, everywhere. Only the regulatory fee and the payment processing rate change by country.

Why do old calculators show different regulatory rates?

Etsy revised the rates on June 22, 2026: France rose from 0.47% to 1.14%, the UK from 0.32% to 0.48%, Italy from 0.32% to 0.80%, and Hungary was added at 1.97%. Many calculators still show the old numbers.
