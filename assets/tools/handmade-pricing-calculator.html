“Materials × 2” alone is the classic underpricing trap

It ignores your time and the platform’s cut. Price the full cost:

  1. Labour is hours × your rate — $20–$40 an hour is typical for most crafts, $50+ for specialised skills.
  2. Overhead is often 5–15% of materials plus labour: packaging, tools, wear, listing time.
  3. On Etsy, about 9.5% + $0.45 of every sale goes to fees — add it on top, or the markup pays Etsy instead of you.

About this tool

What this handmade pricing calculator does

The most-cited beginner formula is (materials + labour) × 2 = retail price, where labour is hours times your hourly rate. The fuller, sustainable version adds overhead and platform fees on top, and the wholesale/retail variant doubles the base cost for wholesale and doubles it again for retail. This calculator runs the fuller form: base cost times your markup, then platform fees, then your profit per item.

A quick example

Materials $25, 2 hours at $25 an hour ($50 labour), $5 overhead: base cost $80. At ×2 the retail price is $160. Etsy fees at 9.5% + $0.45 are about $15.65, leaving a profit of about $64.35 per item.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter materials per item, hours per item, and your hourly rate.
  2. Add overhead per item and set the markup (2 is standard).
  3. Set the platform fee % and read the retail price, the fees and your profit.

Who it is for

Makers, crafters and Etsy sellers pricing handmade items — and anyone who has been charging “materials × 2” and wondering why the work does not pay.

Honest limitations

No official Etsy pricing formula exists — these are trade-standard formulas from craft-pricing guides, not Etsy-published rules. There is no single standard hourly rate for makers; $15–$40 an hour is the range guides quote, and you should use your own. The fee-aware exact form is price = production cost ÷ (1 − fee % − margin %). Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Handmade Pricing Calculator — common questions

How do you price handmade items?

The standard formula is (materials + labour) × 2 = retail price, where labour is hours × your hourly rate. The fuller form adds overhead and platform fees: materials + labour + overhead + profit = retail price.

What hourly rate should a maker charge?

Guides quote $20–$40 an hour for most handmade crafts, and $50+ for specialised skills. There is no single standard rate — use what your time is actually worth, not the lowest rate you can live with.

What are Etsy’s fees on a handmade sale?

About 9.5% + $0.45 per order in the US: $0.20 listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee on price plus shipping, and 3% + $0.25 payment processing.

How do you price for wholesale and retail?

Wholesale = (materials + labour + overhead) × 2, and retail = wholesale × 2. So a $40 base cost wholesales at $80 and retails at $160.

What is the fee-aware pricing formula?

Selling price = production cost ÷ (1 − fee % − margin %). For example, a $53.50 cost with 9.5% fees and a 40% margin needs a price of about $105.94.
