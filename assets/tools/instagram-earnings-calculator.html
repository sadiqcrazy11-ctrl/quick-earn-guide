About this tool

What this Instagram earnings calculator does

An Instagram earnings calculator starts from the industry's rough rule of thumb — about $10 to $20 per 1,000 followers per sponsored post — multiplies by your follower count, then adjusts for engagement, because brands pay for attention, not headcount. An account with 10,000 followers at 2% engagement estimates $100 to $200 per post. The same account at 4% engagement estimates $120 to $240, after the 1.2x engagement multiplier. Monthly earnings multiply the per-post range by sponsored posts per month.

A quick example

Run the defaults: 10,000 followers, 2% engagement (1.0x multiplier), 4 sponsored posts a month. The per-post range is $100.00 to $200.00 and the monthly range is $400.00 to $800.00. Grow to 50,000 followers at 4% engagement with 2 posts a month and the range becomes $600.00 to $1,200.00 per post, or $1,200.00 to $2,400.00 a month. These are planning estimates, not guaranteed income — most accounts sell far fewer posts than they hope.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your followers and your real engagement rate (average likes plus comments, divided by followers).
  2. Set how many sponsored posts you realistically sell per month — be honest, most small accounts sell zero to two.
  3. Edit the low and high rates per 1,000 followers to match quotes you have actually received.

Who it is for

Creators pricing their first brand deal, comparing Instagram with TikTok income, or sanity-checking a brand's low offer.

Honest limitations

Published ranges disagree wildly because they use different tier boundaries and self-reported deals. Finance and tech niches pay more than general lifestyle. Reels usually cost more than static posts, and usage rights or exclusivity should raise the price further. Instagram itself pays creators directly only through gifts, subscriptions, and invite-only bonuses — brand deals are where the money is, and they are never guaranteed. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Instagram Earnings Calculator — common questions

How much does Instagram pay per 1,000 followers?

As a rough rule of thumb, brands pay about $10 to $20 per 1,000 followers for one sponsored post, according to Sprout Social's benchmark. That is an estimate, not a rate card: engagement, niche, and format move real quotes well outside that band.

How much do Instagram influencers make per post?

Published 2026 benchmarks put a sponsored feed post at about $10 to $100 for nano creators under 10,000 followers, $100 to $500 for micro creators, $500 to $5,000 for mid-tier, $5,000 to $10,000 for macro, and $10,000 or more for mega creators. Sources disagree because rates are negotiated and self-reported.

Does engagement rate matter more than follower count?

Yes, for most brands. A small account with 5% engagement often earns more per post than a large account with 0.5%, because the brand is buying real attention. This calculator applies a 0.8x to 1.4x multiplier by engagement band as a planning assumption.

How many sponsored posts can a small account sell per month?

Few. Most accounts under 10,000 followers sell zero to two sponsored posts a month, and many months sell none at all. Enter a realistic number above: a high per-post rate times zero posts is still zero income.
