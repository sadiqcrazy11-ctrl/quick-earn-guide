About this tool

What this fee comparison calculator does

Every marketplace quotes its fee differently — a percent here, a flat fee there, a fee on shipping on one and a fee on the item only on another. Comparing "13.6%" against "10%" against "$2.95" in your head is hopeless. This calculator runs one sale through 15 platforms with their real 2026 fee structures and ranks them by the only number that matters: what stays in your pocket after fees and your costs.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $50 sale, a $20 item, $6 to ship. Mercari and Upwork keep the most at $25.00 each; Amazon keeps the least at $16.50. Same item, same price, $8.50 apart — that is the whole point of comparing before you list. Payment processors (Venmo, Square, Stripe, PayPal, Cash App) are included too, because a direct sale keeps more than any marketplace — but only if you can find the buyer yourself.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter the sale price, your item cost, shipping charged, and what shipping actually costs you.
  2. Read the ranked table — the fee column shows each platform's cut, the "You keep" column is after fees and your costs.
  3. Re-run it at your real prices. High-ticket items punish percentage fees; cheap items punish flat fees.

Who it is for

Resellers deciding where to list, freelancers comparing Fiverr and Upwork, and anyone selling direct who wants to know what a payment app keeps.

Honest limitations

Amazon FBA fees, eBay promoted-listing ads, Etsy offsite ads (12–15%), store subscriptions, and payout timing are not modeled — those can flip a close call. Mercari and Poshmark assume buyer-paid shipping; Fiverr and Upwork assume a service with no shipping. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Fee Comparison Calculator — common questions

Which platform keeps the most from a sale?

It depends on the price. At the $50 defaults, Mercari and Upwork keep the most; at $500, the percentage gaps widen and flat-fee options pull ahead of percentage-heavy marketplaces. Run your own price — the ranking changes with it.

Why does eBay charge a fee on shipping and tax?

eBay's final value fee applies to the total amount of the sale — item price plus shipping charged plus sales tax — not just your price. That is why eBay looks worse here than a flat 13.6% suggests.

Are payment processing fees included?

Yes, where they apply. Gumroad's total includes its 2.9% + $0.30 card processing on top of the 10% platform fee; Depop's 3.3% + $0.45 is processing only, because Depop charges no selling fee. Stripe, PayPal, Square, Venmo and Cash App are processors, not marketplaces — they show what a direct sale keeps.

What is NOT included in this comparison?

Amazon FBA fees, eBay promoted-listing ads, Etsy offsite ads (12–15%), store subscriptions, and each platform's payout timing. Those can flip a close call, so treat this as the fee ranking, not the final decision.
