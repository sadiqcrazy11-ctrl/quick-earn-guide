About this tool

What this Twitch earnings calculator does

Twitch income comes in four streams, and each one splits differently. Subscriptions pay 50% of net revenue at the standard rate — 60% or 70% if you qualify for the Plus Program. Bits pay a flat $0.01 each. Prime subscriptions pay a fixed $2.25 in the US, whatever your share is. Ads pay 55% of net ad revenue. This calculator adds all four from your subscriber counts and shows monthly and yearly totals.

A quick example

Run the defaults: 100 Tier 1 subs, 25 Prime subs, 5,000 Bits a month, $200 of gross ad revenue, standard 50% share. Subs pay $299.50, Prime pays $56.25, Bits pay $50.00, ads pay $110.00 — $515.75 a month, $6,189.00 a year. At the 70% Plus Level 2 share, subs pay $419.30 and the total rises to $635.55 a month.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your subscriber counts by tier — most channels are almost all Tier 1.
  2. Add Prime subs, monthly Bits, and your gross ad revenue for the month.
  3. Pick your real revenue share: 50% standard, 60% Plus Level 1, or 70% Plus Level 2.

Who it is for

Twitch streamers checking what their channel earns, affiliates and partners comparing the Plus tiers, and anyone deciding whether streaming can pay a bill.

Honest limitations

Subscription prices and shares vary by country and currency — these are US dollar figures. Donations, sponsorships, merch, and Twitch's payout threshold ($50) are not modeled. Plus Points depend on paid and Prime subs, gifted subs, and recurring subs — check your own dashboard for your level. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Twitch Earnings Calculator — common questions

How much does Twitch pay per subscriber?

At the standard 50% share, a US Tier 1 sub ($5.99) pays about $3.00, Tier 2 ($9.99) about $5.00, and Tier 3 ($24.99) about $12.50. At the 70% Plus Level 2 share, those rise to about $4.19, $6.99 and $17.49.

What is the Twitch Plus Program?

A revenue-share upgrade for Affiliates and Partners. Level 1 pays 60% when you earn 100 or more Plus Points a month for 3 straight months; Level 2 pays 70% at 300 or more Plus Points a month for 3 straight months.

How much are Twitch Bits worth?

One cent each to the streamer — 5,000 Bits cheered in a month pays $50. Viewers buy Bits from Twitch at a higher price; the streamer always gets $0.01 per Bit.

How do Twitch ads pay?

Streamers get 55% of net advertising revenue when they run 3 or more ad minutes per hour. The gross figure depends on your viewers, your category, and the season — enter your own gross ad revenue for an honest total.
