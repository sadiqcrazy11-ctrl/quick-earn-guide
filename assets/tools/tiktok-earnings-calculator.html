Tool 9 — About this tool

What this TikTok earnings calculator does

This TikTok earnings calculator answers the question every creator asks after a video takes off: the views were real — but what are they actually worth in dollars? It takes your monthly qualified views, applies an RPM (the amount TikTok effectively pays per 1,000 qualified views under Creator Rewards), and turns it into a monthly figure, a yearly figure, and an average per video. The RPM slider deliberately stops at $1.00, because that is roughly the top of what Creator Rewards really pays. Any calculator promising $20+ per thousand views is borrowing YouTube numbers.

A quick example

Run the defaults: 100,000 qualified views at a $0.70 RPM = $70/month. That is $840 over a year, and — if you posted 30 videos — about $2.33 per video. It is real money, and it is also honest money: one decent video does not pay the rent. Try the low end of the slider, $0.40, and the same views pay $40; push your watch time and content quality up, and the high end is where the RPM creeps toward $1.00.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your monthly qualified views — use the qualified-views figure from your Creator Rewards dashboard, not your total profile views, because only qualified views get paid.
  2. Set an RPM. Use $0.70 if you are unsure, $0.40 for a cautious floor, and up to $1.00 only if your dashboard already shows strong watch time on 1-minute-plus videos.
  3. Read your estimated monthly earnings, the yearly equivalent, and the per-video average — then decide if Rewards alone is the plan, or just one slice of it.

Who it is for

Creators inside (or close to) Creator Rewards who want a reality check before scaling, beginners comparing TikTok against YouTube money, and anyone who has been shown a screenshot of '$5,000 from one video' and wants to know what the views alone would realistically have paid.

Honest limitations

RPM varies wildly — by niche, by viewer country, by how long people actually watch. Only 'qualified' views count: original videos of at least 1 minute, watched on the For You feed, and TikTok changes those rules whenever it likes, so re-check them in the app. And keep the bigger picture in view: brand deals and affiliates often pay more than Rewards ever will, and neither is included here. FAQ

TikTok Earnings Calculator — common questions

What is a good TikTok RPM?

Creator Rewards typically pays about $0.40–$1.00 per 1,000 qualified views, based on creator reports — TikTok does not publish an official rate. Longer watch time, original content, and viewers in higher-paying countries push you toward the top of that range; short or recycled clips sit at the bottom.

Do all my views count?

No. Only qualified views count: views of original videos that are at least 1 minute long, watched on the For You feed, from real viewers. Views from ads, duets, stitches, photo posts, or your own profile do not count — and TikTok changes these rules often, so check the rules inside your Creator Rewards dashboard.

Can I live off TikTok Creator Rewards?

Honestly, very few people can. At 100,000 qualified views a month you are looking at about $70 — you would need millions of qualified views every single month to replace a normal income. Most creators who actually earn a living on TikTok combine Rewards with affiliate commissions and brand deals, which usually pay far more.

What about TikTok Shop affiliate earnings?

That is a completely separate program. With TikTok Shop you earn a commission when viewers buy products through your videos or lives — often 5–20% of the price, set by the seller. It can pay much more than Rewards, but it depends on the products you pick and the trust you have built. This calculator covers Creator Rewards only.
