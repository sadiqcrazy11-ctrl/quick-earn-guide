About this tool

What this Patreon vs Substack fee calculator does

Patreon and Substack both take a 10% platform fee, which makes them look identical — until you add the rest. Both charge 2.9% + $0.30 in card processing per payment. Substack adds a 0.7% recurring billing fee on top. Patreon adds a payout fee ($0.25 to a US bank, or 1% via PayPal) and keeps legacy rates of 5%, 8% and 11% for older pages. This calculator runs your revenue through the full stack and shows monthly net, net per subscriber, and the margin.

A quick example

Run the defaults: $1,000 a month from 100 subscribers at $10 each, US bank payout. Patreon standard takes $100 platform + $59 processing + $0.25 payout and keeps you $840.75 a month — $8.41 per subscriber. Substack takes $100 platform + $59 processing + $7 billing fee and keeps you $834.00 — $8.34 per subscriber. At this size Patreon keeps $6.75 more a month; a legacy 5% Patreon page would keep $890.75.

Honest limitations

Processing assumes US card and Apple Pay payments at 2.9% + $0.30; PayPal and Venmo payments outside the US cost 3.9% + $0.30. Currency conversion (2.5% on Patreon), taxes, refunds, and chargebacks are not modeled. Assumes one payout a month. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Patreon vs Substack Fee Calculator — common questions

What does Patreon take in 2026?

A 10% platform fee on pages published after August 4, 2025, plus 2.9% + $0.30 card processing per payment, plus a $0.25 payout fee to a US bank (or 1% via PayPal). Legacy pages keep Founders 5%, Pro 8%, or Pro + Merch 11%.

What does Substack take in 2026?

10% platform fee plus Stripe processing of 2.9% + $0.30 per payment plus a 0.7% recurring billing fee. Substack pays out via Stripe with no separate payout fee.

Which is cheaper, Patreon or Substack?

At the same revenue and pledge, Patreon standard is slightly cheaper — by the 0.7% billing fee Substack adds. On $1,000 a month that is $7. The bigger gap is legacy Patreon: a 5% Founders page keeps about $50 more a month than either standard plan.

How do Patreon payouts work?

Creators choose a payout method: $0.25 per payout to a US bank account, or 1% via PayPal with a $0.25 minimum and a $20 cap. Currency conversion adds 2.5% when the payout currency differs.
