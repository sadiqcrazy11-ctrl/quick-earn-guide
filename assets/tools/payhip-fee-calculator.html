About this tool

What this Payhip fee calculator does

Payhip sells digital products with three plans that share the same features — only the price differs. Free Forever costs $0 a month and takes 5% of each sale. Plus costs $29 a month and takes 2%. Pro costs $99 a month and takes 0%. But Payhip does not process payments itself: you connect PayPal or Stripe, and they charge their standard rates on top. This calculator adds the plan fee, the Payhip cut and the processing, and shows your monthly take-home.

A quick example

$1,000 of monthly sales at a $25 average order (40 orders) on the Free plan with Stripe: Payhip takes 5% ($50), Stripe takes 2.9% + $0.30 per order ($41), total fees $91, you keep $909. On Plus the same month costs $20 + $41 + $29 = $90 — about the same. That is why the Free-to-Plus breakeven sits near $967 a month.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your monthly sales and your average order value.
  2. Pick a Payhip plan and your payment processor.
  3. Compare the take-home across plans to find your cheapest one.

Who it is for

Digital product sellers on Payhip — ebooks, courses, templates — and anyone deciding whether Plus or Pro pays for itself.

Honest limitations

All three plans include the same features; only the price differs. There is no annual billing option. Breakevens assume the same processor on both plans. Payhip’s VAT and sales-tax handling is free but the tax itself is still collected from buyers. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Payhip Fee Calculator — common questions

How much does Payhip charge?

Free Forever: $0/month + 5% per sale. Plus: $29/month + 2% per sale. Pro: $99/month + 0% per sale. All three plans have the same features — only the price differs.

Does Payhip charge payment processing too?

Payhip itself does not process payments — you connect Stripe or PayPal, and they charge their standard rates on top of Payhip’s fee on every plan. With Stripe that is 2.9% + $0.30 per order.

When is Payhip Plus cheaper than Free?

At about $967 in monthly sales: the 3-point saving (5% down to 2%) covers the $29 monthly cost. Pro beats Plus at about $3,500 a month, where the 2-point saving covers the extra $70.

Is there a Payhip annual plan?

No — Payhip’s pricing page offers monthly billing only, with no annual option.
