About this tool

What this Mercari fee calculator does

Mercari brought back its selling fee in January 2025, and it is a flat 10% of the item price — nothing else on the seller's side. No listing fee, no payment processing fee, and the buyer pays both the shipping and a separate 3.6% Buyer Protection fee. This calculator takes the 10%, subtracts your item cost, and shows per-sale and monthly net.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $40 item that cost you $15, 10 sales a month. The fee is $4.00, you keep $21.00 per sale — a 52.5% margin — and $210.00 a month. The buyer pays another $1.44 in Buyer Protection fees and the shipping, so your price is not carrying those costs.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter the item price — the fee is 10% of this number only, not of shipping.
  2. Add what the item cost you to see true profit, not just the fee.

Honest limitations

Promoted listings, if you use them, add a fee that is not modeled here. Shipping labels bought through Mercari and any seller-paid shipping offers are excluded — this calculator assumes the standard buyer-paid shipping. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Mercari Fee Calculator — common questions

What is Mercari's selling fee in 2026?

10% of the item price, in effect since January 6, 2025. There is no separate processing fee and no listing fee on top of it.

Who pays shipping on Mercari?

The buyer. Since the January 2025 change, shipping is buyer-paid, so the seller's 10% fee applies to the item price only — not to shipping.

What is the Mercari Buyer Protection fee?

A separate 3.6% fee the buyer pays on top of the item price. It covers the buyer if the item never arrives or is not as described. It does not come out of your payout.

Is there a listing fee on Mercari?

No. Listing an item is free, and there is no monthly fee for a standard seller account.
