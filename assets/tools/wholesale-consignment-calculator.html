About this tool

What this wholesale & consignment price calculator does

Wholesale and consignment are the two ways shops stock handmade goods, and they price differently. In wholesale, the shop buys at the wholesale price and resells — keystone pricing sets retail at twice wholesale, so wholesale is about 50% of retail. The maker’s chain is cost × 2 = wholesale, wholesale × 2 = retail. In consignment, the shop displays the work and the maker is paid only when it sells, splitting the retail price — 60/40 in the maker’s favour is the recommended minimum, 50/50 is more common. This calculator runs both from one cost.

A quick example

Full unit cost $8: wholesale is $8 × 2 = $16, retail is $16 × 2 = $32. On consignment at 60/40, the maker gets $19.20 and the shop keeps $12.80. At 50/50 the maker gets $16.00 — the same as wholesale, but only when the item sells.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your full unit cost: materials plus labour plus overhead.
  2. Set the wholesale and retail markups (both 2 is the standard chain).
  3. Set the maker’s consignment share (60% is the recommended minimum) and compare the payout with the wholesale price.

Who it is for

Makers and craft sellers pricing for shops, and shop owners checking whether a wholesale or consignment offer is fair.

Honest limitations

Keystone is a convention, not a law — it fails when the retail margin is below about 60%, and a straight 50% wholesale discount can lose money. “Wholesale = 50% of retail” and “retail = wholesale × 2” are the same rule stated two ways, not two different rules. Healthy wholesale margin for handmade goods is 25–40% of the wholesale price after fully loaded cost. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Wholesale & Consignment Price Calculator — common questions

What is keystone pricing?

Retail price = wholesale price × 2 — a 100% markup that gives the retailer about a 50% gross margin. For example, a $14 wholesale cost retails at $28.

How do you price handmade goods for wholesale?

The standard maker chain: wholesale price = full cost × 2, retail price = wholesale × 2 — so retail is cost × 4. On an $8 cost that is $16 wholesale and $32 retail. A margin-based alternative is wholesale = full unit cost ÷ (1 − target margin).

What is a fair consignment split?

60/40 in the maker’s favour is the recommended minimum; 50/50 is more common. Shop commission typically runs 30–50%. Never accept less than 50% as the maker.

Wholesale or consignment — which is better for the maker?

Wholesale pays upfront and in full; consignment pays only when the item sells. If the consignment payout is less than the wholesale price, wholesale is the better deal — at 50/50 on a $32 retail price, the maker gets $16, exactly the wholesale price, but later and only if it sells.
