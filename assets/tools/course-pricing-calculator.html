About this tool

What this course pricing calculator does

Course pricing usually starts from the wrong end: pick a price that "feels right," then hope the students show up. This calculator starts from the number that matters — the monthly revenue you need — and works backwards through realistic student counts, your platform's actual cut, processing fees, refunds, and your monthly plan cost to find the price that hits the target. The platform comparison is the point: the same $1,000 goal needs a very different price on Udemy's marketplace (where you keep 37%) than on your own Teachable school or a self-hosted checkout.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $1,000 target, 20 students a month, Teachable Starter at 7.5% plus 2.9% + $0.30 processing, a 5% refund rate, and a $29 monthly plan. You need about $57.55 per student: each sale nets roughly $51.45 after fees, 19 paying students (after refunds) bring in $977.55, and the plan cost leaves about $948.55 — the calculator solves the price so the monthly net lands on your target, plan included.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your monthly revenue target and a realistic student count — use last month's actuals, not launch-week hopes.
  2. Pick your platform. Fee fields fill in from published 2026 schedules; edit them if your plan differs.
  3. Set your refund rate and monthly plan cost, then read the required price — and check whether real students would pay it.

Who it is for

First-time course creators choosing a platform, existing creators repricing, and anyone comparing Udemy's built-in audience against keeping more per sale elsewhere.

Honest limitations

Udemy percentages apply to its "net amount" (what the student paid after taxes and app-store fees), so iOS/Android sales keep less than the headline 37%. Skillshare has no fixed rate at all — its royalty pool pays per minute watched, so the $/student field is your own estimate. Marketing costs are excluded. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Course Pricing Calculator — common questions

How much does Udemy take from instructors?

On marketplace sales with no instructor coupon or link, instructors keep 37% of the net amount and Udemy keeps 63%. When a student buys through your own coupon or referral link, you keep 97%. The percentages apply to the net amount after taxes and app-store fees.

What does Teachable charge per sale?

The Starter plan ($29/month billed yearly) charges a 7.5% transaction fee on top of card processing (about 2.9% + $0.30). Higher plans remove the transaction fee. This calculator defaults to the Starter structure — edit the fields for your plan.

What does Gumroad charge?

10% + $0.50 per direct sale, plus card processing. Sales through Gumroad Discover pay a flat 30%, processing included. There is no monthly plan cost.

How does Skillshare pay creators?

From a royalty pool based on minutes watched by Premium members — there is no fixed per-student percentage. This calculator lets you enter your own expected earnings per student instead of pretending a fixed rate exists.
