About this tool

What this UGC rate calculator does

A UGC quote has four parts, and beginners usually price only one: the video itself. The expensive parts are usage rights (a video a brand runs as a paid ad is worth far more than one it simply posts), exclusivity (being barred from competitor work costs you other income), and rush delivery. This calculator builds the quote the way working creators do — deliverables first, usage fee on the video portion, then exclusivity and rush uplifts — and returns a range from the published 2026 beginner band of $50–$150 per video, so you can see the floor and the reasonable ask side by side.

A quick example

Run the defaults: one video with 30–90 days of paid-ad usage. The base is $50–$150, the usage fee adds 40% ($20–$60), and the suggested range is $70–$210, midpoint $140. Add exclusivity and rush and the same video quotes $105–$315. Quote the usage line separately in your email — it is the line that stops a brand running your face in ads for a year at a one-video price.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Count your deliverables: videos, photos, and extra hooks or variations. Photo and hook rates are editable starting points.
  2. Pick the usage rights honestly: organic-only is free of uplift; paid ads cost the brand more, and longer terms cost much more.
  3. Add exclusivity and rush if they apply, then quote from the range — new creators start near the low end, proven portfolios near the high end.

Who it is for

Beginner UGC creators pricing their first brand deals, creators who have been handing over ad rights for free, and anyone checking whether a brand's offer is fair.

Honest limitations

Rates vary by niche, brand size, and portfolio strength — the $50–$150 band is a reported beginner range, not a law. Usage multipliers beyond 90 days are typical market practice, not published standards. FAQ

UGC Rate Calculator — common questions

How much should a beginner UGC creator charge?

Published 2026 pricing guides put beginners at $50–$150 per short video, with a median asking rate around $150. Usage rights are separate: roughly 30–50% extra for 30–90 days of paid-ad use. Start near the low end, raise your rate as your portfolio grows.

What are UGC usage rights?

The permission a brand buys to use your video — where (organic posts vs paid ads), for how long, and on which platforms. Paid-ad usage costs more because the video directly generates sales. Always quote usage as a separate line and put the duration in writing.

Should I charge extra for exclusivity?

Yes. If a brand bars you from working with its competitors, that costs you other income for the exclusivity period — a 25–50% uplift is typical. Without an exclusivity fee, you are giving away future earnings for free.

Do I need followers to do UGC?

No. Brands buy the finished video, not your audience. Three good practice videos matter more than a follower count — which is why this is one of the few side hustles where starting from zero followers is normal.
