About this tool

What this print-on-demand profit calculator does

Print-on-demand looks like free money until the cost stack lands: the base product, shipping to the customer, the platform's cut, and payment processing — all before a dollar of profit. A $24.99 t-shirt with an $11 base cost and $4.75 shipping has already spent $15.75 before fees. This calculator runs the full stack per sale, shows your real margin, and converts a monthly goal into the sales count it demands, so "I need 500 sales a month" stops being a surprise.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $24.99 tee, $11 base, $4.75 shipping, no shipping charged, 9.5% + $0.45 in fees ($2.82). Profit per sale is $6.42 — a 25.7% margin. A $500 monthly goal needs 78 sales. Switch to a hoodie at $49.99 with a $21 base and $6 shipping and the same fee structure leaves $17.79 per sale — the product choice matters more than the design.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Pick a product — base cost and shipping fill in with typical 2026 ranges. Replace them with your provider's exact quote for your blank and print areas.
  2. Enter your selling price and what you charge for shipping (0 if shipping is "free" — the cost still counts).
  3. Set the fee fields to your platform: the default mirrors Etsy-style costs; a Shopify store with card processing is closer to 2.9% + $0.30.

Who it is for

Etsy and Shopify POD sellers pricing their first products, designers checking whether a niche can pay, and anyone whose POD payout was thinner than expected.

Honest limitations

Base costs vary by blank brand, print provider, size, print areas, and discount plans — the defaults are mid-range starting points, not quotes. Ad spend, design time, returns, and platform subscriptions are excluded. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Print-on-Demand Profit Calculator — common questions

How much profit is there in a print-on-demand t-shirt?

At typical 2026 costs — $8–$13 base, $4–$7 shipping, ~10% in platform and payment fees — a $24.99 tee leaves roughly $5–$9 profit. Hoodies and higher-priced items leave more per sale because the fixed costs do not grow with the price.

Is Printful or Printify cheaper?

Printify is usually cheaper per unit because it routes orders through many independent providers (tees from about $8.11, hoodies from $19.86, mugs from $5.02 in 2026 catalog checks), while Printful prints in-house with more consistent quality at slightly higher base prices. Both change prices often — check your exact product before pricing.

Should I offer free shipping on POD products?

Only if the price absorbs it. Shipping costs $4–$7 per order either way; 'free shipping' just moves that cost into your margin. This calculator counts the shipping cost whether or not you charge the buyer for it.

What margin should I target on print-on-demand?

Aim for at least 30% after all costs if you plan to run ads, since ad spend comes out of the same margin. Under 20% leaves no room for a discount sale, a return, or a slow month.
