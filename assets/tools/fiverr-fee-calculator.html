About this tool

What this Fiverr fee calculator does

A Fiverr fee calculator applies the platform's flat 20% seller fee to an order. Fiverr's Help Center states the rule plainly: for every order you complete, you earn 80% of the purchase amount, including Gig Extras and tips. So a $100 order credits $80 to your earnings, and a $20 tip credits $16. The buyer separately pays a service fee of 5.5% plus $3.50 on orders under $200, which never comes out of your share. Reverse mode divides your target take-home by 80% to find the list price.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $100 order with no tip. Fiverr keeps $20.00 and you keep $80.00, while the buyer pays $109.00 ($100 + 5.5% + $3.50). Add a $20 tip and the order total is $120: Fiverr keeps $24.00 and you keep $96.00. Flip to reverse mode and ask to keep $500, and the calculator says list $625.00, because $500 ÷ 0.80 = $625.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Pick a mode: what you keep from a price, or what to charge to keep a target.
  2. Enter the order price and any tip. Extras belong in the price, since the fee covers them too.
  3. Read your take-home, the Fiverr fee, and what the buyer pays in total.

Who it is for

Freelancers pricing Fiverr gigs, comparing Fiverr with direct clients, or checking why a payout is smaller than the order total.

Honest limitations

FAQ

Fiverr Fee Calculator — common questions

What percentage does Fiverr take from sellers?

Fiverr takes a flat 20% of every completed order, including Gig Extras and tips, at every seller level. You earn 80% of the purchase amount. That rule is stated on Fiverr's Help Center earnings page.

Does Fiverr take 20% of tips too?

Yes. The 20% fee applies to the full purchase amount including tips, so a $20 tip credits $16 to your earnings and $4 to Fiverr. The buyer also pays their own service fee on the tip payment.

How much does the buyer pay on top of my price?

The buyer pays a service fee of 5.5% of the purchase amount, plus $3.50 on orders under $200. On a $100 order the buyer pays $109.00 in total. None of that fee comes out of your earnings.

What price should I list to take home $500?

Divide your target by 0.80: $500 divided by 0.80 is $625. List $625 and, after Fiverr's 20% fee of $125, you keep $500 before withdrawal charges and taxes. Use reverse mode above for any target.
