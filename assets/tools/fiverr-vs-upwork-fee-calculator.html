About this tool

What this Fiverr vs Upwork fee calculator does

Fiverr and Upwork look similar until the payout lands. Fiverr takes a flat 20% of everything — the order, the Gig Extras, even the tip. Upwork takes 10% on contracts formed before May 2025, and a variable fee between 0% and 15% on newer contracts, set per contract and shown to you before you submit a proposal. Then Upwork adds Connects at $0.15 each and a withdrawal fee. This calculator puts both payouts side by side and tells you what to charge on Fiverr to net the same as Upwork.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $500 job, Upwork at 10%, no Connects, a $0.99 ACH withdrawal. Fiverr takes $100 and keeps you $400. Upwork takes $50 plus $0.99 and keeps you $449.01 — $49.01 more. To net the same $449.01 on Fiverr, you would need to charge $561.26.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter the job amount as the client sees it.
  2. Pick your Upwork contract type — older contracts are a flat 10%; newer ones need your exact rate from the contract.
  3. Add the Connects you spent and your withdrawal method to see Upwork's true take-home.

Who it is for

Freelancers pricing the same work on both platforms, sellers deciding where to list a service, and anyone whose Upwork contract rate is not 10%.

Honest limitations

Fiverr's withdrawal fees and currency conversion are not modeled, and neither are Upwork's membership plans or optional boosts. The variable Upwork rate is set per contract — two freelancers can pay different rates on similar jobs, so only the rate on your own contract is real. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Fiverr vs Upwork Fee Calculator — common questions

What is Fiverr's fee in 2026?

A flat 20% of every completed order, including Gig Extras and tips. On a $500 order, Fiverr keeps $100 and you keep $400, before withdrawal costs.

What is Upwork's fee in 2026?

A flat 10% on contracts formed before May 2025, and a variable service fee between 0% and 15% on newer contracts. The rate is set per contract, shown before you submit a proposal or accept an offer, and locked once the contract is sent.

Which keeps more, Fiverr or Upwork?

Usually Upwork, by about 10 points on the same job — $49 more on a $500 job at a 10% Upwork rate. But a 15% Upwork contract and Fiverr's 20% are only 5 points apart, and Connects plus withdrawal fees narrow the gap further.

How do I price the same job on both platforms?

Divide your target take-home by 0.80 for Fiverr. To net $449 on Fiverr you charge about $561. This calculator does that math for you in the last row.
