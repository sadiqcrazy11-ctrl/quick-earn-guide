Pinterest is the cheap reach — if the intent fits

Pinterest CPC runs about half of Meta’s. Make it pay:

  1. Expect CTR of 0.5–1.5% on standard pins — lower than Facebook, because Pinterest users browse, not click.
  2. Food, home and fashion CPCs run $0.30–$0.70; finance runs $0.80–$1.50. Judge yours against your vertical, not the average.
  3. The US bid floor for a click campaign is $0.10 — bids below that never serve.

About this tool

What this Pinterest Ads calculator does

Pinterest sells ads by auction, with no rate card and no published averages. The honest way to judge a campaign is the same standard math as any ad platform: CPM is spend divided by impressions times 1,000, CPC is spend divided by clicks, CTR is clicks divided by impressions, and ROAS is revenue divided by spend. This calculator runs all five from your numbers, with 2026 ranges to compare against.

A quick example

$300 of spend, 60,000 impressions, 400 clicks, 25 conversions and $690 of revenue: CPM $5.00, CPC $0.75, CTR 0.67%, cost per conversion $12.00, ROAS 2.3x — right on the 2026 sales median.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your ad spend and the impressions, clicks and conversions it bought.
  2. Enter the revenue those ads produced.
  3. Read CPM, CPC, CTR, cost per conversion and ROAS — and compare each with the 2026 ranges below.

Who it is for

Ecommerce sellers, bloggers and creators advertising on Pinterest, and anyone comparing Pinterest’s costs with Facebook Ads.

Honest limitations

Every benchmark here is a third-party estimate — Pinterest publishes no CPC or CPM averages, and vendors disagree: CPM estimates run from $2 to $10. Treat the ranges as planning guides, not prices. Pinterest is consistently cheaper than Meta on CPC (about half) and CPM (about a quarter lower) in cross-platform comparisons. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Pinterest Ads Calculator — common questions

How much do Pinterest Ads cost?

There is no fixed price — Pinterest charges by auction. Third-party 2026 estimates put CPC at $0.50–$1.50 (average about $0.83) and CPM at $2–$10. The US bid floor for a click campaign is $0.10.

What is a good ROAS on Pinterest Ads?

Benchmarketing’s median for sales campaigns is 2.3x, with the middle half of advertisers between 1.1x and 4.0x. But “good” means above your own break-even ROAS, which depends on your margin.

Are Pinterest Ads cheaper than Facebook Ads?

On CPC and CPM, usually yes — Pinterest CPC runs about 48% of Meta’s and CPM about 26% below Meta’s in 2026 cross-platform comparisons. Pinterest CTR is lower though, so cost per conversion is the fair comparison.

What is CPV on Pinterest?

Cost per video view — about $0.15–$0.30 in 2026 estimates. Video pins also carry a lower CTR (0.3–0.5%) than standard pins.
