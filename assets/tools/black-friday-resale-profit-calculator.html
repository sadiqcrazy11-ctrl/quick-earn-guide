About this tool

What this black friday resale profit calculator does

A black friday resale profit calculator answers the question every flipper gets wrong at first: after the marketplace takes its cut and the box ships, what did I actually make? The buy price is only the start. This calculator subtracts the marketplace fee on the full sale, any promoted-listing ad fee, the shipping you pay, and packaging — then shows profit, margin, and return on the money you put in.

The fee rules come from this site's verified fee pages. eBay charges 13.6% of the total sale up to $7,500 and 2.35% above it, plus a per-order fee. Poshmark charges a flat $2.95 under $15 and 20% at $15 and up. A Facebook Marketplace sale paid through PayPal Goods & Services costs 2.99%. A local cash sale costs nothing in fees — but reaches far fewer buyers.

A quick example

Run the defaults: buy for $40 on Black Friday, resell on eBay for $100, pay $8 shipping and $2 supplies, no ads. The eBay fee is 13.6% of $100 = $13.60 plus the $0.40 per-order fee = $14.00. Total costs are $64.00, so profit is $36.00 — a 36.0% margin and a 90.0% return on the $40 you put in. Sell the same item on Poshmark for $40 after buying it for $15 with $1 of supplies: the fee is 20% = $8.00 and profit is $16.00. Drop the resale price to $10 and the flat $2.95 fee takes 29.5% — profit on a $2 item is only $5.05.

How to use it

  1. Pick the marketplace you will sell on. The fee fields update to that marketplace's verified rates — edit them if your account differs.
  2. Enter the Black Friday buy price, the resale price, and every cost: shipping you pay and packaging.
  3. Read the profit per flip. If it is under $5, one return or price drop wipes it out.

Who it is for

Resellers flipping Black Friday deals on eBay, Poshmark, or Facebook Marketplace, and anyone deciding whether a doorbuster deal is worth buying in quantity.

Honest limitations

The eBay fee base includes sales tax collected from the buyer, which this calculator does not model — if you collect tax, add it to the shipping-charged field so the fee base is right. Returns, damaged items, storage, driving to the post office, and income tax on profits are not included. Marketplace fee schedules change; the fields are editable so you can match your account.

FAQ

Black Friday Resale Profit Calculator — common questions

How much does eBay take when I resell a Black Friday deal?

For most categories, 13.6% of the total sale (price + shipping charged + sales tax) up to $7,500, then 2.35% on the portion above, plus a per-order fee of $0.30 on orders of $10 or less and $0.40 over $10. Promoted listing ads add your chosen ad rate on top.

Is reselling Black Friday deals legal?

In general, yes: once you buy an item, you may resell it. That does not make every flip safe — some brands restrict new-in-box reselling on certain platforms, clearance items may be final sale with no return if your buyer complains, and income from reselling is taxable. Check the platform's rules and keep receipts.

Which marketplace keeps the most profit on a flip?

It depends on the price. Local cash keeps 100% but reaches few buyers. PayPal Goods & Services at 2.99% beats eBay's 13.6% on the same price, but eBay brings the buyers. Poshmark's flat $2.95 fee punishes cheap items: on a $10 sale it takes 29.5%. Run the same numbers on each marketplace in this calculator and compare.

What costs do new resellers forget?

Shipping you pay, packaging and tape, the fee on the shipping you charged, promoted listing ads, and returns. A $36 profit on paper can drop under $20 after a return label and a replacement box. Enter every cost honestly — the calculator only knows what you type.
