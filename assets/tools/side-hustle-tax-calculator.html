About this tool

What this side hustle tax calculator does

Side income has a nasty surprise built in: nobody withholds tax from it, and it is taxed twice. First comes self-employment tax — 15.3% on 92.35% of your net self-employment earnings, covering the Social Security and Medicare contributions an employer would normally split with you. Then comes ordinary federal income tax on what is left, after the standard deduction and after deducting half of the SE tax. This side hustle tax calculator runs both layers with the real 2026 figures, so the number you set aside is based on arithmetic, not on a rule of thumb someone posted.

A quick example

Take the defaults: $30,000 of 1099 income, $2,000 of expenses, no W-2 wages, filing single. Net self-employment income is $28,000, the SE tax base is $25,858, and SE tax comes to about $3,956. After deducting half of that and the $16,100 standard deduction, federal income tax is about $992. Total estimated federal tax: about $4,948 — roughly $1,237 a quarter, or about 17% of gross income to set aside from every payout.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Pick your filing status and enter your 1099 income, any W-2 wages, and your business expenses.
  2. Split business miles between the two halves of 2026 — the IRS raised the standard mileage rate mid-year, from 72.5 cents to 76 cents a mile.
  3. Read your SE tax breakdown, your income tax estimate, your quarterly payment, and the set-aside percentage to apply to every payout.

Who it is for

Freelancers, gig drivers, delivery couriers, Etsy sellers, and anyone with 1099 income who has ever reached April owing more than expected. It is especially useful in your first year of side income, when there is no prior return to copy estimates from.

Honest limitations

This is a federal-only estimate. It does not include state or local income tax, the qualified business income (QBI) deduction — which can lower taxable income for many pass-through businesses — tax credits, or retirement contributions, all of which can move the real number. It also assumes the standard deduction; if you itemize, your income tax will differ. If your numbers are large or complicated, a tax professional earns their fee quickly. FAQ

Side Hustle Tax Calculator — common questions

How does the side hustle tax calculator work?

It subtracts your business expenses and mileage deduction from your 1099 income to get net self-employment income, applies the 15.3% self-employment tax to 92.35% of that (with the Social Security wage base and the 0.9% additional Medicare tax), then runs the remainder through the 2026 federal brackets after the standard deduction and the deductible half of SE tax. Estimates only — not tax advice.

Why is the mileage rate split into two halves of 2026?

The IRS raised the standard mileage rate in the middle of the year: 72.5 cents per mile for January 1 to June 30, 2026 (Notice 2026-10), and 76 cents per mile for July 1 to December 31, 2026 (Announcement 2026-11). Splitting your miles gets the deduction right instead of guessing one rate for the whole year.

Do I really have to pay tax quarterly?

If you expect to owe $1,000 or more in tax that is not covered by withholding, the IRS generally expects quarterly estimated payments (Form 1040-ES) — and can charge an underpayment penalty if you wait until April. Paying quarterly also spreads the pain: four smaller payments hurt less than one big one.

What does this calculator leave out?

State and local income tax, the QBI deduction, tax credits, retirement contributions, and any W-2 withholding your employer already took. It is a federal planning estimate, not a return. Treat the set-aside percentage as a floor, and check the real figures with a tax professional before you rely on them.
