About this tool

What this Vinted fee calculator does

Vinted charges sellers nothing: no listing fee, no commission, no subscription. You keep 100% of the item price. The platform makes its money from the buyer, who pays the Vinted fee at checkout — usually 5% of the item price plus a $0.70 fixed fee in the US — plus shipping. This calculator shows both sides, so you can price knowing what the buyer actually pays.

A quick example

A $40 item with $5 shipping: the buyer pays a $2.70 Vinted fee (5% of $40 is $2.00, plus $0.70), so their total is $47.70. You still receive the full $40.00.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter your item price.
  2. Enter the shipping the buyer pays.
  3. Read what the buyer pays in total — and confirm you keep 100% of the price.

Who it is for

Anyone selling secondhand clothes and items on Vinted in the US, and resellers comparing Vinted’s 0% seller fee with other resale apps.

Honest limitations

The 5% + $0.70 buyer fee is what Vinted calls “usually” — the exact fee varies by item, bundle and order and is shown at checkout. UK fees usually run 3–8% + £0.30–£0.80. Item Bump and Wardrobe Spotlight prices are shown per item at checkout; Vinted publishes no fixed price for them. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Vinted Fee Calculator — common questions

Does Vinted charge sellers a fee?

No. Vinted states there are no selling fees: no listing fee, no commission, no subscription. Sellers keep 100% of the item price.

What is the Vinted fee?

The fee buyers pay at checkout — usually 5% of the item or bundle price plus a $0.70 fixed fee in the US. It covers secure payment processing, buyer protection and support. It is never deducted from the seller’s payout.

How do I get paid on Vinted?

Earnings go to your Vinted Balance once the order completes — when the buyer confirms everything is OK, or 2 days after delivery with no issue reported. Withdraw to a linked bank account (minimum $0.10, up to 5 business days).

What do Item Bump and Wardrobe Spotlight cost?

They are paid visibility features: a Bump lifts one item for 3 or 7 days, Spotlight lifts your whole wardrobe for 7 days. Vinted publishes no fixed price — the cost depends on the item and duration and is shown at checkout.
