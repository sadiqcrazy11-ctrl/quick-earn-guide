About this tool

What this Square fee calculator does

Square charges a percentage plus a fixed fee on every card payment, and both numbers move with your plan and with how the card is taken. In person on the Free plan it is 2.6% + 15¢; online it is 3.3% + 30¢; keyed in by hand it is 3.5% + 15¢ — on every plan. Plus ($49 a month per location) and Premium ($149) shave the in-person and online rates, but the monthly fee has to be earned back. This calculator shows the per-sale fee, the monthly total with your plan, and whether your plan actually saves money versus Free at your volume.

A quick example

Run the defaults: a $50 in-person sale, Free plan, 100 transactions a month. The fee is $1.45 per sale, you keep $48.55, and the month nets $4,855. Switch to Plus and the fee drops to $1.40 — but the $49 monthly fee means the month nets $4,811, which is $44 worse than Free at this volume. Plus only wins once your card-present volume passes about $49,000 a month.

How to use it — 3 steps

  1. Enter a typical sale amount and how the card is usually taken.
  2. Pick your plan — or the plan you are considering — and your monthly transaction count.
  3. Read the "Your plan vs Free" row: positive means the upgrade pays, negative means Free is cheaper.

Who it is for

Market-stall sellers, salons, cafés, and online sellers deciding whether Square Plus or Premium is worth the monthly fee.

Honest limitations

International cards add 1.5% on all plans and are not modeled. Gift-card load fees, hardware costs, and add-on software are excluded. Plan features (inventory, loyalty, reporting) are worth something too — this calculator only judges the processing math. Estimates only — not financial advice.

FAQ

Square Fee Calculator — common questions

What are Square's fees in 2026?

In person: 2.6% + 15¢ on Free, 2.5% + 15¢ on Plus ($49/mo), 2.4% + 15¢ on Premium ($149/mo). Online or invoice: 3.3% + 30¢ on Free, 2.9% + 30¢ on Plus and Premium. Keyed-in or card on file: 3.5% + 15¢ on every plan.

Is Square Plus worth $49 a month?

Only at volume. Plus saves 0.1% on in-person sales, so it needs about $49,000 a month in card-present sales to cover its fee on processing alone. Online it saves 0.4%, so about $12,250 a month in online sales covers it. Below that, Free is cheaper.

Does Square charge a monthly fee?

The Free plan costs $0 a month. Plus is $49 a month per location and Premium is $149 a month per location. There is no setup fee on any plan.

Why do keyed-in cards cost more?

A card typed in by hand, or charged from a saved profile, carries more fraud risk because neither the card nor the cardholder is present — so Square charges 3.5% + 15¢ for it on every plan, and no upgrade lowers it.
